Lifetime residuals

The core of the plan

You earn 50% of the profit on every merchant you board, every month, for the life of the merchant. It's guaranteed — whether you take a break from the space or move on entirely, as long as the merchant processes through NextPay, you get paid.

How it works

  • 50% of profit, every month
  • Paid for the life of the merchant
  • Yours even if you step away

Why it compounds

  • Low buy rates — more of every deal's profit stays yours
  • Transparent residual reporting
  • Every merchant you keep adds to next month's check

The math that matters: a book of 30 merchants averaging $100/mo profit each pays you $1,500/mo — forever — before a single bonus.

Lead programs

Earned by closers · buy-in optional

Three programs keep your pipeline full. Live Transfers and NextPay Paid Ads are earned: you're eligible after your first closed deal, they're fed to top-performing reps, and you keep receiving them as long as you keep closing. NextLink is our LinkedIn outreach program. Want full, immediate access to any of them? All three can be purchased at agent pricing (ask for current rates).

1 · Live Transfers

  • Vetted, high-intent leads transferred live to your phone
  • Fed to top performers — eligible after your first deal
  • Keep closing to keep them coming

2 · NextPay Paid Ads

  • Paid social campaigns run for you — targeted local ads
  • Inbound merchant interest fed to top performers
  • Eligible after your first deal — keep closing to keep them

3 · NextLink (LinkedIn outreach)

  • Multichannel outbound: LinkedIn, email, voice & video
  • Averages 30–60 new lead conversations a day
  • Available at agent pricing

Promotion path

3-month milestone

The monthly target is 5+ qualifying deals in a calendar month. Hit it three straight months and raise your hand — we'll negotiate a promotion with a better split and bigger bonuses. Top agents grow with us; the standard plan is the floor, not the ceiling.

* All merchants must be processing a minimum of $30,000/month in sales to qualify. If the merchant signs a contract, an exception to the volume minimum may be possible.

Track it: log your deals in My Dashboard and price every deal from Pricing & Fee Programs. Ready to board one? Submit a Deal.
