Buy Rates & Schedule A Agents only

Every product boards through a different partner, so each has its own Schedule A — your cost basis. Pick a product to see its buy rates. On every line you can pass it through at cost, mark it up, or absorb it in your residual to close.

How to read this: the buy rate is your cost. What you charge the merchant above it — and what you absorb — is your call, deal by deal. Estimate the split on the Residual Calculator and build the quote in the Proposal Builder.
