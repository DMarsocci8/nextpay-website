Square

Best for: simple retail, cafés & low-volume, tech-comfortable merchants

What it is: the ultra‑easy, self‑serve POS + payments platform. A truly free base plan, no monthly minimum, and a merchant can be live the same day. Brand everyone already trusts.

Pricing snapshot

Free $0/mo (2.6% + 10¢ in‑person) · Plus $29–89/mo by vertical · Premium contracted. Hardware: Reader $59, Terminal $299 (or $27/mo), Handheld $399 ($37/mo), Stand/Station $149–899. Financing available. Flat‑rate processing — Square keeps the rate, so it's the merchant who pays; dual‑pricing is limited.

Where Square wins

  • Fastest, easiest setup anywhere — merchant can self‑install, DIY option is $0
  • True free base plan; no monthly minimum; predictable flat rate
  • Huge brand trust; slick app, online store, invoices, loyalty all in one
  • Great for pop‑ups, mobile and seasonal — pay only when they swipe

Where Square falls short

  • Flat rate gets expensive as volume grows — the merchant eats it; weak on dual pricing / 0%
  • Known for holding/freezing funds on risk flags
  • Shallow for full‑service restaurants & complex multi‑location
  • iOS‑centric; support is remote/self‑serve

Pitch Square when…

  • The merchant wants zero commitment, to start today, and to set it up themselves
  • Low ticket / low volume where flat rate is fine and dual pricing isn't the goal
  • Retail, coffee, quick service, mobile vendors, side‑hustles graduating to a real POS
  • They already know and trust the Square brand — don't fight it, place it

Clover

Best for: retail & QSR wanting great hardware + an app for everything

What it is: Fiserv‑owned all‑rounder — polished hardware (Station, Mini, Flex, Go), a big app market, and reliable payments. Works for restaurants, retail, services and more. We can place it outright or on the Placement Program (no upfront, monthly placement).

Pricing snapshot

Hardware bought outright ~$1,766–2,173 per station bundle, or Placement (no upfront, monthly). Software plans ~$14.95–$120/mo by vertical; add‑on devices ~$9.95/mo. Peripherals: cash drawer ~$164, receipt printer, scanners priced per proposal. We control the processing — traditional, cash discount or dual pricing all work.

Where Clover wins

  • Best-in-class hardware & form factors; strong brand recognition
  • Flexible — retail, restaurant, services; huge app market fills gaps
  • Placement Program removes the upfront-cost objection
  • We own the processing — great for dual pricing / cash discount deals

Where Clover falls short

  • App‑market model stacks monthly fees — "cheap" base gets pricey
  • Not purpose‑built or deep for complex full‑service restaurants
  • Hardware is proprietary — locked to Clover if they leave us
  • Rate schedules vary wildly by channel — be the honest one

Pitch Clover when…

  • Retail or QSR that wants premium hardware and room to add apps later
  • The merchant balks at upfront cost — lead with the Placement Program
  • You're setting up dual pricing / cash discount and want proven hardware
  • They want one recognizable ecosystem for POS, online ordering & loyalty

Quantic

Best for: restaurants & retail wanting deep features at a low monthly

What it is: a flexible cloud POS with a deep add‑on catalog for both restaurant and retail — KDS, online ordering, loyalty, inventory, scales, self‑order. Support is included with the software and referral pricing is aggressive.

Pricing snapshot

Software from ~$10–$25/mo per register (referral pricing, support included); hardware bundles ~$1,119–$1,469. Rich add‑on catalog (KDS, CDS, scales, printers, scanners) each priced modestly per month. We control processing — traditional or dual pricing.

Where Quantic wins

  • Very deep feature set for the price — punches above low monthly
  • Does both restaurant & retail well (scales, self‑order, KDS, inventory)
  • Support included; strong referral pricing keeps merchant cost down
  • Flexible add‑on catalog — build exactly what they need

Where Quantic falls short

  • Lower brand recognition — you'll have to sell the name
  • Deep catalog = more to configure; needs a proper build
  • Less turnkey than Square for a merchant who wants dead‑simple

Pitch Quantic when…

  • Feature-hungry restaurant or retail that wants a low monthly, not a big SaaS bill
  • They need scales, self‑order kiosks, KDS or deep inventory without Toast pricing
  • Value shoppers who care more about capability than a famous logo
  • You want margin room and full control of the processing

KORONA

Best for: liquor, convenience, vape/tobacco, ticketing & franchises

What it is: a retail‑first POS built for high‑SKU stores — liquor, convenience, vape, and ticketing/franchise operations. Billed per register per month with strong inventory, age verification and multi‑store reporting.

Pricing snapshot

Software ~$50/mo per register (tiers to ~$59–$99/mo for Plus/Franchise features); hardware from the KORONA dealer sheet ~$1,400–$2,600 per bundle. We control processing — traditional or dual pricing.

Where KORONA wins

  • Purpose‑built for high‑SKU retail — deep inventory, purchase orders, vendors
  • Age verification & case‑break handling for liquor / vape / convenience
  • Strong multi‑store & franchise reporting; ticketing module
  • Flat per‑register software — predictable as they add lanes

Where KORONA falls short

  • Overkill for a simple, low‑SKU merchant — sell it where inventory is the pain
  • Not a restaurant POS — don't force it into full‑service dining
  • Lesser‑known brand; setup rewards a thorough inventory import

Pitch KORONA when…

  • Liquor store, smoke/vape shop, convenience or grocery with thousands of SKUs
  • They need real inventory control, age checks and vendor/PO management
  • Multi‑location or franchise that wants consolidated reporting
  • Ticketing / event venues needing that module

NRS

Best for: bodegas, corner stores & independent convenience

What it is: National Retail Solutions — the bodega & convenience‑store POS. One flat software price, cash‑discount processing built in so the merchant pays $0 per transaction, plus its own ad/loyalty network on the customer‑facing screen.

Pricing snapshot

One flat ~$49.95/mo software price; cash‑discount processing = $0/transaction to the merchant. Terminal bundle typically placed low or free with the program. Simple, one‑line deal.

Where NRS wins

  • Built exactly for the bodega / corner store — nothing to over‑explain
  • Flat monthly + $0/txn cash discount is a dead‑simple pitch
  • Customer‑facing screen earns ad/loyalty revenue
  • Cheap, sticky, and easy to place volume

Where NRS falls short

  • Narrow — it's a convenience POS, not for restaurants or deep retail
  • Cash‑discount model only — merchant must be OK passing the fee
  • Basic hardware & feature depth vs. Clover/Quantic

Pitch NRS when…

  • Independent convenience store, bodega, corner deli or small market
  • Merchant wants the simplest possible flat fee and $0 processing cost
  • They're fine with cash discount / customers covering the fee
  • Price is the whole objection — this is your low, sticky placement

DejaPay Pro

Best for: straightforward merchants who want 0% via dual pricing on solid hardware

What it is: Dejavoo's station‑based POS — a clean, no‑nonsense system on Dejavoo hardware, built around dual pricing so the merchant processes at 0%. Great when they want a real POS station but the headline is "pay nothing to process."

Pricing snapshot

~$59/mo software on Dejavoo hardware; built around dual pricing → 0% effective processing for the merchant. Simple station bundle, no sprawling app market to price out.

Where DejaPay Pro wins

  • Dual pricing is the whole product — 0% processing is an easy headline
  • Solid Dejavoo hardware we know and support
  • Simple, station‑based — quick to quote and install
  • Low, flat monthly — no app‑market surprises

Where DejaPay Pro falls short

  • Not as deep/flexible as Clover or Quantic for complex operations
  • Best where the merchant is committed to dual pricing
  • Lesser‑known name — you're selling the 0% story, not a brand

Pitch DejaPay Pro when…

  • Merchant's #1 ask is "how do I stop paying processing?" — lead with 0% dual pricing
  • They want a genuine POS station, not just a terminal, but nothing over‑built
  • Straightforward retail or service business with standard needs
  • You want a clean, fast‑to‑close dual‑pricing deal on hardware we support

PAYS POS

Best for: merchants wanting one all‑inclusive 0% bundle, tiered by features

What it is: an all‑inclusive dual‑pricing POS — one station bundle, tiered software plans, and 0% processing for the merchant. Everything packaged together so there's one price and one story.

Pricing snapshot

Station bundle around $999; tiered software $59 / $79 / $99 per mo by feature level; dual pricing → 0% effective processing for the merchant. All‑inclusive — hardware + software + 0% in one package.

Where PAYS wins

  • All‑inclusive — one bundle, one price, 0% processing story
  • Clean good/better/best tiers ($59/$79/$99) make it easy to quote
  • Great for merchants who want everything decided for them
  • Dual pricing baked in from the start

Where PAYS falls short

  • Bundled approach = less à‑la‑carte flexibility than Clover/Quantic
  • Committed to dual pricing — not for a merchant who won't pass fees
  • Newer/smaller brand — sell the package, not the logo

Pitch PAYS when…

  • Merchant wants the easy button — one all‑in bundle with 0% processing
  • You want simple good/better/best tiers to close fast
  • Standard retail or service that doesn't need a deep custom build
  • Dual pricing is a yes and they'd rather not piece hardware together
