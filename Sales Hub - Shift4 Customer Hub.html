SkyTab by Shift4 Customer Hub

The merchant-facing portal that supports a restaurant through every stage of its SkyTab by Shift4 journey — from onboarding and setup to daily operations and ongoing growth. Use this to explain what happens after the sale and how the merchant runs their business from anywhere.

Getting started Onboarding Everyday use Marketplace Resources
Customer Hub URL lh.shift4.com Merchants manage their SkyTab by Shift4 system remotely from a PC or smartphone — they're no longer tied to a physical location.

Getting started

Once a merchant application is submitted, an email is automatically sent to the address on file prompting the merchant to create their Customer Hub account and begin setup.

Onboarding — before go-live

New SkyTab by Shift4 merchants are taken to their first onboarding screen, which walks them through the crucial steps to be set up for success. Progress auto-saves, so they can resume where they left off — setup takes about 10 minutes.

After onboarding, the merchant lands on the Grow My Business home, which opens the full Customer Hub via the left-hand navigation (launch progress, recommended products like Kiosk & Handheld, Shift4 Capital financing, and tutorials).

Marketplace

Customer Hub includes access to Marketplace — native and third-party apps that let a business do more than just manage the POS. Merchants simplify operations (run services through the POS), save time on admin & reporting, and reduce costs by automating processes. With Marketplace they build a more connected operation and get more value from SkyTab by Shift4. Offerings depend on the merchant's region/market — full list: shift4.com/marketplace.

How Marketplace helps merchants

  • Connects the restaurant tools they already use with SkyTab by Shift4
  • Cuts duplicate work by letting systems talk to each other
  • Opens integrations — online ordering, inventory, payroll, accounting, loyalty & more
  • Keeps front-of-house and back-of-house more connected
  • Adds features & services that grow with the business

How Shift4 vets apps — talk track

A secure API connects third-party apps to SkyTab by Shift4 while protecting customer data. Every app passes a rigorous review:

  • Must be "Best in Class"
  • Its best pricing is offered through the Marketplace
  • Must not have competing processing

Shift4 apps (native)

  • Online Ordering — integrates with the menu; includes QR Ordering for contactless dine-in ordering & payment at no extra charge
  • QR Pay — secure contactless payment; guests scan a code to pay, integrated with the POS/PMS

Online ordering & delivery

Branded pickup/delivery beyond the dining room. Once configured on Workstation, orders flow straight from the app to the device — no re-keying into the POS.

DoorDash · Uber Eats · GrubHub · Menufy · OpenTable · Maple · Eatsy & more

Payroll & HRM

Track wages, streamline payments. Connected to Workstation, employee & labor data flows between systems — less manual entry, more accurate payroll.

7Shifts · Flexspring

Accounting

Sync sales & financial data from the POS into the accounting platform — less manual reconciliation.

Restaurant365 · DAVO Sales Tax · Shogo · Buyers Edge · Wenodo

Inventory

SkyTab by Shift4 has basic inventory reports; these add advanced control — physical counts, waste tracking, recipe control & inventory-health metrics. Menu/sales data is sent over, so no re-entry.

MarginEdge · Marketman · Craftable · Backbar · BeerBoard · Nory · Yellow Dog

Property management

For hospitality — guest & transaction info moves between systems to coordinate dining, room charges & guest experiences across locations.

Oracle Opera 5 · Opera Cloud · Ezee

Installing an app

Integration via Marketplace is built-in and ready to go — in most cases it's one click.

1
Open Marketplace

From the Customer Hub home, pick Marketplace in the left-hand nav.

2
Browse & view details

See all available apps; click View Details for pricing, brochures & docs.

3
Install & allow

Click Install → Allow. The integration page shows exactly what data Shift4 shares.

4
Manage

The button flips to Manage and the app appears in the Marketplace left nav.

Install from the app's details page inside Marketplace — not the public shift4.com/marketplace site. Already have an account with the app? On the prompt, check "My restaurant has a business account with…" to connect directly.
