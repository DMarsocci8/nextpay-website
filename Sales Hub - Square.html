Software plans

Per location. All plans include a 30-day free trial.

Square Free

$0 /mo per location
30-day free trial
  • POS app for any payment
  • Online site & item library
  • Invoicing & booking
  • Checking & savings accounts

Square Plus

$49 /mo per location
30-day free trial · unlimited devices
  • Everything in Free
  • Industry POS features & lower processing fees

Square Premium

$149 /mo per location
30-day free trial · unlimited devices
  • Everything in Plus
  • 24/7 priority support · advanced reporting
  • Lowest processing fees
  • More text marketing · no gift card load fees

Custom rate (anything different than rack rate): merchant must process over $500k annually, with custom pricing approval and a 2-year contract signed by the seller.
