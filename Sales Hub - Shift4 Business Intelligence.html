Business Intelligence

Shift4's analytics tool (SkyTab Business Intelligence / STBI) gives merchants complete data transparency — accessed right inside the Shift4 Dine Customer Hub. Here's what it does, why it wins, and how to get it turned on.

What it is Advantages Key features Enabling it

What it is

Business Intelligence (BI) — SkyTab Business Intelligence (STBI) — is Shift4's analytics platform. It goes beyond traditional reporting to give merchants complete data transparency with fast, interactive dashboards. Merchants reach it directly through the Shift4 Dine Customer Hub.

Three competitive advantages

  • Unparalleled data transparency and speed
  • Easily configurable dashboards
  • Group By and Drill To features to improve data mining

It is not "traditional reporting" — that's the trap answer.

Key features

KPIs

KPI = Key Performance Indicator — the metrics merchants track to measure business performance.

Group By

Changes the entire view of the report being examined, organized by the group data the merchant specifies.

Drill To

Lets a merchant focus on a single line of data within a report for a closer look.

Hierarchies

Merchants can use up to 3 hierarchies to structure how their data rolls up.

Data transparency

BI has complete data transparency — a core advantage over traditional reporting.

Enabling it for a merchant

Submit the request form

To get BI enabled in LBM (Lighthouse Business Manager), complete the SkyTab BI Merchant Request form for each merchant. It is not added automatically.

Access via Customer Hub

Once enabled, the merchant accesses Business Intelligence through their Shift4 Dine Customer Hub.

Do not share the demo login. The login information for the demo version of STBI must not be shared with merchants.
