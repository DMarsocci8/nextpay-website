## 2) Online Ordering

**Why it matters:** Pizza Hut claims its first **online food order** was placed in **1994**. The average diner now uses online ordering apps as a **discovery tool** (e.g., Uber Eats) to find where to eat — it's about reach.

**Terms:**
- **Ghost kitchen** = a restaurant that offers **delivery-only** options (no dine-in space).
- **Food delivery app** = lets customers browse restaurants & order delivery (DoorDash, Uber Eats, GrubHub).
- **Gig worker** = an **independent contractor** who gets paid for **each job** they complete (e.g., delivery driver paid per delivery).

**Managing online menus in Lighthouse (BMS) — three features:**
- **Menu Manager** = configure online ordering menus; choose whether each item is offered online. **Auto-syncs with the POS menu.**
- **Online Price** = change the menu price for items offered online (separate from in-store price).
- **Schedules** = select which **day of week and time** items are available online.

**Shift4 Dine online ordering:** native Online Ordering + QR Ordering at no extra charge; third-party apps connect via the **Marketplace**. Orders flow straight to the device — no re-keying.

**Online Ordering quiz answer key (verbatim):**
- Ghost kitchen → **delivery-only restaurant** · First online order 1994 → **Pizza Hut** · DoorDash → **food delivery app**
- Menu Manager lets you choose if an item is offered online → **TRUE** · Configure online menus → **Menu Manager**
- Change an online price → **Online Price** · Customers use apps as a **discovery tool** · Menu Manager auto-syncs with POS → **TRUE**
- Gig worker → **independent contractor paid per job** · Day/time availability → **Schedules**

## 7) Gift Card Program

**Migration & setup:**
- **Jan 2022**, Shift4 began converting merchants from **It's Your Card (IYC)** → **The Customer Connection's (TCC)** platform.
- Pilot = **single-owner** locations; then expanded to **multi-location** merchants.
- The new portal lets **merchants place their own gift card orders** in **Lighthouse Business Manager** (previously Shift4 staff did it manually) and gives them a central place to manage gift cards.

**Gift Card Setup Wizard (first order = wizard):**
1. **Card Types** — choose E-Gift (virtual), physical, or both.
2. **Gift Card Store** — name the store + create a **unique URL** (e.g., `mygiftcard.s4shops.com/gc/`); links the program to LBM. **Required even if not selling online.** URL must be **≥ 4 characters**.
3. **Add Logo** — optional; can be skipped or updated later.
4. **Select Colors** — primary & secondary; changeable later.
5. **Go live** — store is live; then prompted to **Select Physical Card Design** and **Order Inventory**.

**E-Gift vs. Physical:**
- **E-Gift Cards:** sold online; emailed to recipient; funds **instantly available**; redeemable at POS via **QR code or keying**.
- **Physical Gift Cards:** sold online or in-store; receive notice of new orders; fulfill at POS; mail to customer; redeemable at POS by **swipe, QR code, or keying**.

**Accepting gift cards:**
- Eligible merchants get an **email** with setup instructions for LBM.
- **Physical, in person:** process with the attached **MSR (magnetic swipe reader)** or **manually enter** the number.
- **E-gift, in person:** **scan the QR code** in the customer's email, or **manually enter** the number.
- **Online:** the customer enters the gift card number on the **merchant's website payment screen**.
- **Legacy IYC cards are still accepted** after switching to TCC.

**Designing & ordering:**
- Design your own card **or** pick from the **library**; **only one design per order** (multiple designs = separate orders).
- Design-your-own: upload an image, upload logo, add text, pick a background color; preview **front & back**; then select **quantity, shipping method, address**.
- Mailed within **10–15 business days** (**15–20** during the holiday season).
- For a **physical** card bought **online**, the **merchant loads the amount and ships** it.

**Pricing:** **60-day free trial** + optional **50 free physical gift cards**; then **$12.95/month + $0.20 per transaction**.

**Merchant benefits (the pitch):** immediate **revenue return in advance** of providing products/services; improved **word-of-mouth** advertising from loyal customers; an **ease-of-use dashboard** to create designs, re-order physical cards, and maintain existing cards & balances.

**Gift Card Program assessment answer key (verbatim):**
1. Where can a merchant place a gift card order? → **Lighthouse Business Manager** (not InCharge App, not Marketplace, not Lighthouse Transaction Manager)
2. Physical gift card bought online — merchant loads the amount and ships it → **TRUE**
3. Both formats must be refilled by calling the Merchant Support phone line → **FALSE**
4. E-gift card bought online — how does the customer get funds? → **Emailed a QR code**
5. Merchants can accept gift cards online and in person → **TRUE**
6. Benefits Shift4 provides (select all) → **Immediate revenue return in advance**, **Improved word-of-mouth advertising by loyal customers**, **Ease-of-use dashboard to create designs / re-order / maintain cards & balances** (NOT "free physical gift cards forever")
