Advantage Program — 0% card fees

Shift4's dual-pricing program shows a cash price and a card price. Customers who pay by card cover a small program fee, so your card-processing cost is offset to effectively 0% and you keep the full ticket. It's a compliant dual-pricing / cash-discount program — clearly disclosed at checkout, not a hidden surcharge.
