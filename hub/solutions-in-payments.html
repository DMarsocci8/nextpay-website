How to Submit an Application

Follow these steps inside the Solutions in Payments Portal to submit a new merchant application.

  1. Log in to the portal

    Use your Solutions in Payments credentials. If you don't have access, request it from the support line.

  2. Click "Submit New Application"

    This launches the merchant application form.

  3. Fill in merchant details

    Business name, address, owner information, EIN, and contact details.

  4. Select Equipment/Gateway type

    Choose from the dropdown: Quantic, Korona, NRS, Chively, Dejavoo, Valor, Ingenico, PAX, FluidPay, NMI, iPOSPays, Authorize.net, Luqra, or others. This determines which placement the merchant is on.

  5. Choose application type & fees

    See the sections below for detailed guidance on which application type to choose.

  6. Review PCI fees & residuals

    Decide whether to waive or bill PCI fees to residuals, and select your breach insurance option.

  7. Upload documents

    Attach merchant voided check, ID, processing statements (if Interchange), and any other required docs.

  8. Verify you're listed as Agent

    Make sure your name appears as the submitting Agent on the deal before hitting send.

  9. Submit & save confirmation

    Hit submit and save the confirmation number for your records.

Step 3: Choose Application Type & Fees

This is where you decide the merchant's pricing model. Each type has different fee structures and profit margins for NextPay.

Recommended

Cash Discount

3–4% recommended

Merchant offers a discount to customers who pay with cash. Credit card processing fees cover the cost. Higher percentage = higher NextPay margin.

Recommended

Dual Pricing Credit + PIN Debit Passthrough Fees

3–4% recommended

Two pricing tiers: one for credit cards, one for PIN debit transactions. PIN debit fees pass through from the processor. Higher credit card percentage = higher margin.

Recommended

Dual Pricing with PIN Debit Flat Rate

3–4% recommended

Same as above, but PIN debit has a flat rate (not passthrough). The flat rate percentage matches the dual pricing credit percentage. Higher = more NextPay revenue.

Good Option

Surcharge

3% recommended

Merchant adds a surcharge to credit card transactions instead of taking a cash discount. Cleaner for merchants who don't want to advertise a discount.

Complex

Interchange Plus

Requires statements

Merchant pays Interchange + mark-up (usually 0.1–0.5%). Lowest transparent pricing but requires their most recent 3-month processing statements to beat/match their current rates.

⚠️ Interchange CRITICAL: Do NOT take the merchant's word that they have a good rate or that they pay "X percentage." 99% of the time they are wrong about their actual costs. You MUST get their current most recent 3-month processing statements. Only the statement shows their true effective rate based on their unique credit card mix and transaction types. Always calculate effective rate from statements, never trust their claims.

Step 5: PCI Fees & Breach Insurance

In the Miscellaneous Fees section, you'll find the PCI fee option.

  • PCI Fee Option: Choose to either WAIVE the fee or BILL it to the merchant's residuals
  • If billing to residuals: Select either Monthly or Yearly "includes breach insurance"
  • Breach insurance: Protects the merchant in case of a data breach. It's automatically included with the PCI fee if chosen.
  • Best practice: For most merchants, waiving PCI fees makes for a cleaner deal. If billing, yearly is more cost-effective for the merchant.

    • Before You Hit Send

    • Documents uploaded: Voided check, ID, processing statements (if Interchange)
    • You are listed as Agent: Verify your name appears as the submitting Agent on the deal
    • All required fields complete: Business info, owner details, EIN, contact info
    • Application type & fees set: Double-check the pricing model and percentages
    • Save confirmation number: After submit, save the confirmation for your records
