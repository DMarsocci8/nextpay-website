What Next2Pay is in the NextPay lineup

  • Invoicing: Send professional invoices, track payment status, collect online via link. Integrates with QuickBooks.
  • Gateway / Virtual Terminal: Card-not-present processing (phone orders, online, keyed). High-ticket, phone-heavy businesses.
  • Recurring Billing: Subscriptions, membership billing, retainers. Card-on-file, auto-charge with dunning (retry failed payments).
  • NextLink (optional add-on): Client automation outreach — SMS + email sequences to clients. Separate licensing model.
  • Margin: House deal — best economics in the book. No revenue share with external provider.

Conversion & positioning

  • If merchant already uses Next2Pay: No conversion — they're locked in. Upsell additional seats, modules (NextLink), or move processing to our rails if applicable.
  • If prospect is NOT using Next2Pay: Assess their workflow: Do they invoice? Take phone orders? Need subscriptions? Next2Pay is modular — bundle only what they need. Lead with "No app to learn, integrates with your tools (QuickBooks, Zapier, etc.), and best margin for us means better pricing for you."

Rate conversation

Next2Pay handles both software (seat-based) and processing. For high-ticket merchants, interchange-plus shines — show the per-transaction breakdown vs their current flat rate. For subscription businesses, the card-on-file + dunning logic (automatic retry on failed charges) often pays for itself in recovered revenue.

Common docs every Next2Pay deal needs (COMPLETE FILE REQUIRED)

  • ✓ Signed Next2Pay application
  • ✓ Last 3 months processing statements (if existing processor)
  • ✓ Last 3 months business bank statements
  • ✓ Articles of Incorporation (LLC, C-Corp, etc.)
  • ✓ Owner's driver's license (copy)
  • ✓ Bank account details (for ACH verification + payouts)
  • ✓ Plaid connection screenshot (open banking verification)
  • ✓ Veriff completion (ID verification)

Incomplete files delay approval. Collect everything upfront — don't submit with missing docs.
