Budget-friendly POS platform — lowest monthly software cost ($0–$99/mo). Perfect for price-sensitive small merchants and pop-ups.

What SumUp is in the NextPay lineup

  • POS Lite: Entry-level — $499 one-time, $0 monthly. Mobile app on your phone/tablet. No recurring fees, lowest total cost of entry.
  • POS Solo: Midrange — $699 one-time. Standalone device, no phone/tablet required. Better for fixed counters.
  • POS Pro: Full POS — $799 one-time, $99/mo plan. Inventory, staff management, multi-register support.
  • Terminals: SumUp Terminal ($249 one-time). Portable card reader for pop-ups, markets, food trucks.
  • Best for: Price-conscious boutiques, pop-ups, food trucks, small retail. The "zero monthly" pitch wins on objection.

Key pitch: "Zero monthly software fees" — merchants already paying $50–$100/mo elsewhere can justify SumUp's upfront cost in 5–10 months.

Conversion & positioning

  • If merchant already uses SumUp: They own the hardware (not placement). No hardware conversion. Upsell: move processing to our rails if not already done, or upgrade software tier (Lite → Pro).
  • If prospect is NOT using SumUp: Lead with "Zero monthly software cost." Calculate total cost vs Square ($0 upfront but $50–$99/mo): "You save $X/year on software, and we beat or match processing rates."

Rate conversation

SumUp merchants are price-sensitive. Dual pricing works exceptionally well: "Zero monthly software + zero card fees on cash = maximum margin for you." If they're on Square (flat-rate) or another processor, show the math: total monthly cost (software + processing) — we usually win on either software cost or rates.
