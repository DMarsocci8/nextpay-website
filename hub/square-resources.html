Everything you need to sell, price, and close Square deals — with step-by-step workflows and templates.

What Square is in the NextPay lineup

  • If a merchant is already on Square: we can only convert them if they're on rack rate with another reseller — we convert them by switching them to cash discount.
  • If a prospect is not on Square yet: sell the hardware + software plan normally, and quote our processing (dual pricing, interchange-plus, or flat rate) on our rails underneath it. Quote the processing — either rack rate or cash discount — with one of the applications below.

Rate conversation, if applicable

There is no rate conversion — let the merchant know the Square rate shown above. If they're already on Square, you can convert them to cash discount. Nothing more to discuss on rates with Square.

Submit a Square deal

1

Pick the hardware

Choose hardware from the lineup above that fits their setup. They'll typically pick this once they go through the application — that's part of why it helps to have gone through the application yourself, so you're familiar with the flow.

2

Confirm the rate plan

Software: Free plan or Plus $49/mo. Processing: quote our rate (dual pricing, interchange-plus, or flat rate on our rails).

3

Submit

Email the order + docs to hello@nextpaypos.com — subject line 'Square deal — {DBA}'. Be sure to do this. Have the merchant email you confirmation once their order is placed / account is set up.

4

Log it

Log the deal in the CRM the moment you start working it — track stage, next step, and close date.

Email template to merchant
