What Square is in the NextPay lineup

  • If a merchant is already on Square: we can only convert them if they're on rack rate with another reseller — we convert them by switching them to cash discount at 4%.
  • If a prospect is not on Square yet: They choose one of two pricing models (see below), sign up through that link, and get their account + application. No negotiation — the rate is the rate. 4% cash discount OR rack rate (IC+). During underwriting, they can request rates between 3-4% if needed.

Rate conversation — NO negotiation

Square rates are fixed and public. There is no "meet or beat" negotiation, no rate conversation, no playing with pricing. Merchant chooses: 4% cash discount OR rack rate (IC+). That's it. If they're already on Square, pitch converting them to cash discount at 4%.

Exception: During underwriting, rates between 3-4% can be requested — that's it. Keep this simple: tell them the rate, move on.

Submit a Square deal

1

Choose the pricing model

4% Cash Discount or Rack Rate (IC+) — that's the choice. Present both, let merchant pick. No negotiation.

2

Send signup link

Merchant chooses their pricing model and signs up through the corresponding link. This creates their account AND starts their application. They select hardware during signup.

📊 Rack Rate (IC+) Signup

3

Gather docs & submit

Collect docs (see checklist below). Email order + docs to hello@nextpaypos.com — subject line 'Square deal — {DBA}'. Square will handle account activation & hardware shipment.

4

Log it

Log the deal in the CRM immediately — track stage, next step, go-live date.

Square Software & Features Included

All Square plans include access to these features. Features vary by industry type — some are industry-specific add-ons.

  • Text Message Marketing — send promotions & updates to customers
  • Gift Card Load — issue & manage gift cards in-store
  • Bitcoin Conversions — accept crypto payments
  • Square KDS App — kitchen display system for food businesses
  • Square Kiosk App — self-service ordering kiosk
  • ACH Bank Transfer (via invoice) — direct deposit payouts
  • ACH Bank Transfer (via API) — programmatic payouts
  • Cash App Afterpay — buy now, pay later integration

Industry-specific features: More advanced features available based on restaurant, retail, salon, or other industry type.
