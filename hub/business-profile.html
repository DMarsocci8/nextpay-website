Set up your Google Business Profile

Your profile puts you in Google Maps and local results for searches like "credit card processing near me" — in your metro. It takes about 15 minutes. Follow this exactly; Google suspends profiles that break the rules.

Before you start: use your @nextpaypos.com Google account if you have one (any Google account works). Have your phone ready — verification is usually a short video where you show you're a real business.

The setup, step by step

Go to business.google.com → "Add your business". Business name: exactly NextPay Business Solutions. Never add your city to the name ("NextPay Dallas" will get the profile suspended).
Category: type payment and pick "Payment terminal" as your primary — it's the closest match Google offers and hits card-machine/POS searches. Then add secondaries under Edit profile: Business to business service, Payroll service, E-commerce service, Financial consultant. (Categories must be picked from Google's dropdown — free-typed text won't save.)
Choose "No" for a storefront (you're a service-area business). Your address stays hidden — Google just needs it to place you. Use your real home/office base, never a PO box or virtual office.
Service areas: add your city and the surrounding towns you actually cover — up to 20, all within your metro. Don't add whole states or faraway cities; it doesn't help ranking and looks spammy.
Website: use YOUR city landing page from the table below — not the homepage. Phone: your work cell.
Verify. Google usually asks for a short video: show your business card or NextPay gear (a terminal box works great), and something tying you to your area. Do it in one take; approval takes a few days.
After it's live: add 3–5 photos (you, equipment, a merchant install), fill the Services list (POS systems, card terminals, invoicing, dual pricing, payroll), and turn on messaging if you'll answer it.
Reviews are the #1 local ranking factor. In the profile, tap Share → "Get more reviews" and save that short link. Text it to every happy merchant at install + 30 days. Never buy or fake reviews — respond to every real one.

The rules that keep your profile alive

  • Real base only — home office is fine; virtual offices, co-working mail addresses and PO boxes get suspended
  • Exact business name, no keywords or cities added
  • One profile per person/market — don't create duplicates
  • Photos and posts are yours; pricing and program claims must match the website (dual pricing, match-or-beat — nothing beyond it)
  • Never screenshot hub pricing into your profile or posts

Backlinks & local citations — the 20-minute play for your market

Links from real local sites are what move you up in Google and get you cited by AI assistants. Do these once for your metro; use your city landing page (table above) as the link wherever possible.

  • Claim your local profiles — Yelp (biz.yelp.com), Alignable, Apple Business Connect (businessconnect.apple.com). Business name exactly NextPay Business Solutions, same description as your Google profile, link to your city page.
  • Your metro's chamber of commerce — membership includes a directory listing. Link it to your city landing page.
  • Merchant footer links — at install and at the 30-day check-in, ask: "If you have a website, would you add a small 'Payments by NextPay' link in your footer, pointing to nextpaypos.com? Helps other local businesses find us." Five real merchant links beat fifty directories.
  • Partner sites — every CPA/bookkeeper/broker you work with should list NextPay as their payments partner, linking to nextpaypos.com/partners (we list them back).
  • Your own LinkedIn — put your city landing page in your profile's website field and link it when you post.
Never buy links or "SEO packages" — purchased link blasts get the site penalized. Real listings and real customers only.
