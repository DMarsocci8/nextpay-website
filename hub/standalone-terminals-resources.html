What standalone terminals are in the NextPay lineup

  • PAX A-series terminals: Smart terminals (A920, A920 Pro, A920Max, A77). Android-based, app ecosystem, WiFi/LTE, battery backup.
  • Dejavoo terminals: Rugged, outdoor-friendly. P Line (portable), QD Line (countertop). Best for food trucks, outdoor events, construction sites.
  • Valor terminals: All-in-one with screen. Vl series. Good for bars, restaurants that want simple terminal + receipt printer (no full POS).
  • Use case: Merchants who don't need inventory/staff management but want smart payment processing. HVAC contractors, service techs, high-ticket sellers, cash-heavy businesses with simple needs.
  • Processing: Our processing (dual pricing, interchange-plus, flat rate). No third-party processor lock-in.

Who buys standalone terminals

  • High-ticket merchants: HVAC, plumbing, medical practices, jewelry repair. Terminal + receipt printer, no POS software needed. Keep their own books or use external accounting.
  • Field service: Technicians who collect on-site (Uber model). Terminal + mobile app or VPN access to home office gateway.
  • Pop-ups & events: Dejavoo P Line for outdoor markets, food trucks, festivals. Rugged, battery-powered, offline mode.
  • Bars & simple restaurants: Valor terminal + receipt printer. No inventory/staff features, just payment processing.
  • Cash-heavy & control-conscious: Business owners who want payment processing without "big software" oversight. Terminal stays in their control, our processing underneath.

Conversion & positioning

  • From Square/SumUp terminal: Pitch our terminal + processing as total cost alternative. "Square terminal limits you to Square processing. We give you a smart terminal + choice of processor." Merchants already on card-present see the value immediately.
  • From legacy terminals: "Your old terminal is dumb — no connectivity, no reports. This PAX/Dejavoo connects to cloud, apps, VPN access for remote teams, offline mode, and our rates beat your current processor."
  • New merchant (field service, contractor, pop-up): Position as "Start here — low commitment. Terminal + processing. If you grow into POS later, migrate anytime. We keep it simple until you need complexity."

Rate conversation

Standalone terminal merchants are often on old-school per-transaction or flat-rate plans. Dual pricing resonates: "Keep the terminal, get zero fees on cash, pay rate X on cards." If CNP (phone orders, online) needed, that's a gateway add-on — quote separately so they see the modular approach.

Standalone terminal fast facts for your pitch

  • No POS lock-in: Merchant keeps their books, CRM, accounting system. Terminal is just payment processing.
  • Smart terminal = app ecosystem: PAX runs apps (loyalty, inventory light, VPN client for remote teams). Not just payment.
  • Offline mode: WiFi down? Terminal keeps working. Batch syncs when connection returns. Critical for remote/outdoor merchants.
  • Dual pricing leverage: Standalone merchants often have cash mix. "Zero fees on cash" is huge ROI for them.
  • Field service favorite: Dejavoo P Line runs on battery, rugged, survives jobsites. Contractors/plumbers/HVAC love it.
  • Total cost: Terminal (one-time) + gateway (if CNP, monthly) + processing (per transaction). Compare to competitor terminal lock-in (Square = Square processor only).
