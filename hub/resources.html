❓ Merchant FAQ — answers you'll give weekly

"Is dual pricing legal?"Yes — posted cash/card pricing is legal nationwide when signage and receipts are done right (that's why the file build matters). Surcharging (a % added at the register) has state-specific rules — that's a different program.
"How long does approval take?"Standard-risk: usually 1–3 business days with a complete file. High-risk or missing docs: longer — which is why we package everything up front.
"When do I get my money?"Typically next business day after batch. Batch time is set on the terminal file.
"What if the equipment breaks?"Placement hardware (SkyTab, Clover placement) carries replacement/warranty — SkyTab hardware has a lifetime warranty. Purchased hardware carries the manufacturer warranty.
"Am I locked into a contract?"Depends on the program — be straight: SkyTab is a 30-day trial then 30-month agreement; house terminal/gateway deals are typically month-to-month. Never dodge this question.
"What about chargebacks?"We help respond with evidence, and chargeback protection programs exist for exposed merchants — see the public page.
"Do you support EBT / gift cards / online ordering?"Yes — program-specific. Check the product picks in the Navigator so you promise the right thing on the right platform.

🎯 Lead generation

  • Referrals from live merchants — the highest-close source there is. Ask after the first settled batch, every time (script in the Playbook).
  • Route clustering — work one plaza/street per block, same industry where possible; openers get sharper each door.
  • DM-to-Sale — 3 opens a day using the DM playbook; local businesses check who engages.
  • New-business signals — "opening soon" signs, new permits, marketplace listings for used POS gear (a seller is a switcher).
  • Company programs — warm live-transfer and LinkedIn lead programs exist for qualifying agents; ask Dom about current availability.
