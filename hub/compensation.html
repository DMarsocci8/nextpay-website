How you get paid at NextPay: residuals for life, bonuses per deal, and a promotion path. Your exact split and Schedule A are in your signed agent agreement — this page is the structure.

The split, in one line: agents earn 50% of the Schedule A — half of whatever each program's schedule pays on the deal. On SIP low-risk paper that's 45% of the gross margin (30% high-risk). Worked example on Pricing → SIP Schedule A. Your signed agent agreement is the final word.

How the money flows on one deal

The merchant processes

Say $45,000/month in cards on the program you quoted.

Margin is created

What the merchant pays minus our buy-side cost (your Schedule A) minus card-brand cost = the deal's monthly margin. Software/hardware monthlies can add margin too.

You get your split

Your contracted % of that margin, every month, for as long as the merchant processes — even if you step away from selling. Run scenarios on the residual calculator.

Bonuses land on top

Activation bonus when the merchant goes live; payroll deals pay their own bonus; monthly closer promos stack.

Your documents

  • Agent agreement + Schedule A — your split and buy rates. Don't have a copy? Email dom@nextpaypos.com.
  • SkyTab partner Schedule A — reference PDF.
  • Working buy-rate notes — keep your numbers on Pricing & Buy Rates.
  • Residual reports — delivered monthly; questions go to Dom.

The promotion path

Hit your targets three months straight and reach out — we negotiate a better split and bigger bonuses. Top agents grow with us. Track your own run rate: your pipeline stages Submitted → Installed are the scoreboard.

Internal: comp specifics, splits and buy rates never go in front of merchants or into proposals.
