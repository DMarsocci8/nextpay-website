Conversion & positioning

  • If merchant already uses SkyTab: They're locked in (36-month agreement). No conversion play. Focus on upselling modules or moving processing to our rails if not already done.
  • If prospect is NOT on SkyTab yet: Pitch the full system — hardware (placement), software modules, processing on our rails. Lead with $0 upfront + lifetime warranty as differentiators vs competitor hardware costs.

Rate conversation

SkyTab is a placement: focus on total cost of ownership (hardware $0 down vs competitor upfront costs), then quote processing separately. Dual pricing works well in casual FSR; fine dining often prefers clean interchange-plus. Lead with the math: "You save [X] on hardware alone, then we match or beat your current processing rate."

SkyTab fast facts for your pitch

  • Lifetime hardware warranty: Any hardware fails, Shift4 replaces it free — for life. Competitive advantage over purchased POS systems.
  • Pay-at-table: Handhelds reduce table turn time and increase tips (customers see itemized receipt, tip on handheld).
  • $0 upfront: Hardware placement means no capital outlay — cash flow stays with the merchant.
  • KDS (kitchen display system): Replaces printer tickets, reduces order errors and remake costs — calculate this ROI for the merchant.
  • Caller ID: Pizza shops + delivery venues: incoming caller's history pops on screen (if in system) — sales + accuracy boost.
  • OLO (Online Ordering): Your own online ordering (not just 3rd-party app integration) — merchants keep 100% of order value, no commission.
