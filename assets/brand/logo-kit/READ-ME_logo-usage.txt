NEXTPAY BUSINESS SOLUTIONS - LOGO KIT
=====================================
Everything you need to put the NextPay logo anywhere - web, social, email,
print, flyers, business cards, apparel and more. Pick the right file for the
background you are putting it on.

BRAND COLORS
  Navy (dark)     #0C1B2A
  Teal            #14A18C
  Bright Teal     #1FC2A6   (the "Pay" wordmark + accents)
  Always pair the logo with plenty of clear space around it.

QUICK PICK - WHICH FILE?
  On a WHITE / light background ....... 01_Primary-Color  (navy Next + teal Pay)
  On a DARK / navy background ......... 02_White          (white Next + teal Pay)
  One-color print / dark shirt ........ 03_Mono-AllWhite
  One-color print / light surface ..... 04_Mono-AllNavy
  Black-only (stamp, fax, engrave) .... 05_Mono-Black
  Where transparency is not allowed ... 06_Solid-Background (PNG + JPG)
  Just the globe icon ................. 07_Icon-Mark-Only
  Social profile pictures (square) .... 08_Social-Avatars-Square
  Website favicon / app icons ......... 09_Favicon-and-App-Icons
  Full lockup with tagline ............ 10_With-Tagline
  Generic web vector wrapper .......... 11_Web-SVG

SIZES
  Each transparent logo comes in large (1600px), medium (800px) and small (400px) widths.
  - Email signature / social post: medium or small
  - Website header: medium
  - Flyers / business cards: large

FILE TYPES
  PNG  - transparent, best for almost everything digital
  JPG  - white/navy background baked in; use where PNG/transparency is not supported
  ICO  - website favicon (multi-size)
  SVG  - included as a web wrapper (raster inside)

APPAREL & LARGE-FORMAT PRINT (banners, signs, embroidery)
  For the sharpest result at large sizes, export a TRUE VECTOR (.SVG, .PDF or .EPS)
  from Canva (Pro: Share > Download > SVG/PDF Print) and give that to your printer.
  The PNGs here are high-resolution and fine for most jobs, but vector never blurs.
