Product Introduction & Hardware

The value proposition, the integrated-payments advantage, the base package, and the SkyTab by Shift4 device lineup — everything you need to introduce the product and pitch the hardware.

The vision

"Deliver an exceptional customer experience with the latest ordering and payment technology."

How we serve: by providing best-in-class POS hardware, merchants can manage their inventory, track employee performance, streamline operations, set up a customer loyalty program, and much more.

SkyTab by Shift4's value proposition

  • Point & click capabilities
  • No upfront costs
  • No-cost software
  • Out-of-the-box, single-vendor offering
  • World-class team for local install & support
  • Best-in-class hardware
  • Engineered for reliability — Shift4 owns the entire software stack: the POS ecosystem and the payment platform.

Payments-integrated hardware

The hardware is sleek with native payments integration — integrated payments lead to a greater customer experience and faster adoption. It's a single-vendor solution: no add-ons or third-party hardware required (unlike competitors), which simplifies development, setup, usage and support.

Devices with native integrated payments: Workstation, Customer-Facing Display, Tablet (Glass) and Mobile.

Base package

Base package · $29.99 / month

The base package is the all-in-one starting point — it includes the POS Terminal (Workstation), 10 Employee Cards, a Receipt Printer and a Cash Drawer, with no upfront cost.

Everything else — Mobile, Glass, KDS, Customer-Facing Display, EMV pin pad, kitchen printers, digital scales and caller ID — is an optional add-on layered on top of the base.

The device lineup

Workstation

The flagship countertop POS with native payments — the heart of the base package.

Customer-Facing Display

A second screen facing the guest for order confirmation, tipping and checkout.

Tablet Glass

A sleek, built-for-purpose tablet that runs a full version of the POS software in the palm of your hand — ideal for merchants who move around the floor.

Mobile Add-on

A handheld for pay-at-the-table and on-the-go. Also available stand-alone as Solo.

Add-on price: $20 / month
