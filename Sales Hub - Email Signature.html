How to add it to your Gmail signature

Do this once on a computer (Gmail's phone app can't edit signatures) — new emails will use it automatically.

  1. Fill in your details above, choose a style, and click Copy signature.
  2. In Gmail, click the gear ⚙ (top-right) → See all settings.
  3. On the General tab, scroll to Signature → + Create new, name it NextPay. (Already have one? Click it and delete what's inside.)
  4. Click in the box and paste: Ctrl+V (Mac: Cmd+V).
  5. Under Signature defaults, set NextPay for new emails and replies.
  6. Tick “Insert signature before quoted text in replies, and remove the '-- ' line that precedes it” to hide Gmail's little dash.
  7. Scroll down, click Save Changes, then send yourself a test.

Tip: if a paste ever looks plain, use Download .html, open that file, select all, copy, and paste that instead. The logo loads from nextpaypos.com, so it stays small (Gmail rejects oversized signatures) and shows on any background.
