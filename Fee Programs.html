Dual Pricing

Two prices, customer chooses

Every item shows a cash price and a slightly higher card price side by side. Whichever way they pay, you net the cash price.

Cash price (shelf)$100.00
Card price (shown)$103.50
You keep either way$100.00

Most popular & clearest for customers. The card price covers the ~3.5% fee, so processing nets to $0.

Cash Discount

One posted price, cash saves

You post the card price everywhere, then give a discount for paying cash at the register. Card payers fund the processing.

Posted price$103.50
Cash discount−$3.50
Cash customer pays$100.00

Simple single-price signage. The posted price already absorbs the fee, so card sales cost you nothing.

Surcharge

A small fee on credit only

Add a compliant surcharge to credit cards (never debit), capped to card-brand and state limits. Debit and cash pay the base price.

Base price$100.00
Credit surcharge (3%)+$3.00
Credit customer pays$103.00

Debit cards can’t be surcharged, so debit & cash stay at $100. We keep you within the legal caps.
