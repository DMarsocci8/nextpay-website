Why online ordering matters

Online ordering isn't new — Pizza Hut claims it accepted its first online food order back in 1994. What's changed is how customers shop: the average diner now uses online ordering apps as a discovery tool (think Uber Eats) to find and choose where to eat, not just to place an order.

For a restaurant, that means online ordering is about reach — getting in front of new customers and keeping orders flowing beyond the dining room. The merchant's job is to make sure their online menu is accurate, priced right, and available at the right times.

Terms to know

Ghost kitchen

A restaurant that offers delivery-only options — there's no dine-in space; it exists to fulfill online/delivery orders.

Food delivery app

An app that lets customers browse restaurants and order delivery.

DoorDash · Uber Eats · GrubHub

Gig worker

An independent contractor who gets paid for each job they complete — e.g., a delivery driver paid per delivery.

Managing online menus in Lighthouse

In Lighthouse Business Manager (BMS), three features control what shows on a merchant's online menus and when.

Menu Manager

Where merchants configure their online ordering menus — including selecting whether a specific item is offered on their online menus.

Auto-syncs with the POS menu

Online Price

Lets a merchant change the menu price for items they offer on their online menus — separate from the in-store price.

Schedules

Lets merchants select which day of the week and time items are available on the online menu.

Two facts worth remembering: Menu Manager auto-syncs with the POS menu Menu Manager controls whether each item appears online

Shift4 Dine online ordering

Shift4 Dine includes native Online Ordering that fully integrates with the merchant's menu for a seamless experience, plus QR Ordering for contactless dine-in ordering and payment — at no additional charge. For broader reach, merchants can also connect third-party ordering and delivery apps through the Marketplace (DoorDash, Uber Eats, GrubHub, Menufy and more). Once configured on Workstation, an order placed in the app flows straight to the device — no re-keying into the POS.
