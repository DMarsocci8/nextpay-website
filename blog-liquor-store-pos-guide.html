Liquor store POS: age verification, case breaks & keeping margins

What a liquor store actually needs from its register — and the setup we install when compliance and speed both matter.

A liquor store register has three jobs: keep the line moving, keep you compliant on every single sale, and protect margins that are thinner than customers think. Here's what to look for — and the stack we typically install.

Age verification that can't be skipped

Manual birthday math fails on a busy Friday. The fix is an ID scanner wired into the POS so the sale can't complete without a scan or manager override. KORONA POS with a Zebra ID scanner does exactly this — the scan is logged with the transaction, which is what you want if compliance ever comes asking.

Inventory that understands a case isn't a bottle

Liquor inventory lives in multiple units — cases, six-packs, singles. Your POS needs case-break support so receiving a case of 12 correctly adds 12 sellable units, and reporting shows margin per bottle. KORONA's retail tier ($79/register/month) handles this natively; it's the reason it shows up in so many package stores.

Keep more of every sale

Card fees hit hardest on thin-margin categories. Two levers:

  • Dual pricing: shelf tags and the terminal show a cash price and a card price; the customer chooses. Run correctly it takes your processing to 0% — and modern terminals print compliant receipts automatically.
  • Debit-heavy mix: liquor stores see lots of PIN debit, which is cheaper to accept. Make sure your terminal actually routes it.

The stack we install most

  • POS: KORONA (Retail tier) with age verification
  • Scanner: Zebra ID scanner + hands-free 1D Orbit for barcodes
  • Terminal: Dejavoo or PAX with dual pricing enabled
  • Backoffice: purchase orders and vendor tracking from day one
Signage matters: dual pricing is compliant when both prices are displayed clearly. We handle the shelf tags and terminal configuration as part of setup.
