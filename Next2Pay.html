Set it and forget it

Recurring billing that runs itself

Put memberships, retainers and subscriptions on autopilot. Next2Pay charges the card or bank on schedule, retries failures, and emails every receipt — so revenue shows up without the follow-up.

  • Any cadence — weekly, monthly, quarterly
  • Cards on file, vaulted securely
  • Auto-retry keeps collection rates high
Sell plans, not chases

Subscriptions your customers sign up for once

Build a membership, service plan or retainer as a subscription plan — customers subscribe once, and Next2Pay bills the card or bank automatically, tracks MRR, and keeps churn visible.

  • Monthly, quarterly or annual plans
  • Subscriber counts & recurring revenue per plan
  • Fee-free bank (ACH) option on every plan
