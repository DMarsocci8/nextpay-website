Scripts & Templates

Word-for-word scripts organized by product, plus ready-to-send text and email templates. Pick a tab, personalize the [brackets], and go. Every script follows the same flow — open, get to the decision maker, ask leading questions, close for the appointment, handle objections.

📋 New: Discovery Call Playbook — how to approach & what to ask first

Two ways to open: lead with savings (lower fees / free rate review) or lead with rewards (earn points on sales they already make). Rewards is a positive, easy-yes hook — you're offering to give them something, not asking them to switch. Get the foot in the door, then layer on the product script. [brackets] = personalize.

Cold call — rewards-first

Agent: "Hi [name], [your name] with NextPay. Quick question — are you earning any points or cash back on your business sales right now? Almost nobody is. You run thousands through every month and get nothing for it. I help owners turn that into travel and rewards — like a card, but on the money coming in. Worth two minutes?"

Why it works: you're adding value, not threatening their setup. Easy yes → gets you the statement → you find the savings too.

Voicemail — rewards-first

Agent: "Hi [name], [your name] with NextPay — I help local businesses earn points and cash back on the sales they're already making, like a rewards card but on money coming in. Worth a quick heads-up. I'll text you too — reach me at [number]."

Walk-in / text — rewards-first

Agent (in person): "Hey, I'm [your name] with NextPay — random question: are you getting any rewards on your card sales? Most owners aren't, and they're leaving real money on the table every month. I can show you what you'd be earning in about five minutes. Who handles payments here?"

Agent (text/DM): "Hi [name], [your name] from NextPay — quick one: you're probably running thousands in card sales a month and earning zero rewards on it. We fix that — travel/cash back on your sales. Worth a 10-min look? [number]"

Cold call — savings opener

Agent: "Hi [name], [your name] with NextPay Business Solutions. I'll keep it quick — I help [business type] owners pay less to accept cards, and most are surprised once we actually look. Are you the right person to talk processing with?"

Goal: a yes to a free rate review or a 10-minute call. Don't pitch a product yet.

Voicemail

Agent: "Hi [name], [your name] with NextPay — I help local businesses lower what they pay to accept cards, and I think there's room to save you money. No pressure; I'll send a quick text too. Reach me at [number]. Thanks!"

Under 15 seconds. Always follow a voicemail with a text.

Walk-in / door (in person)

Agent: "Hey, I'm [your name] — I work with businesses on the block to cut card processing costs. I'm not here to sell you anything today; grab a recent statement and I'll tell you in two minutes whether I can save you money or not. Who handles that here?"

Disarm first. Ask for the statement or the owner's best time — don't pitch in the doorway.

Gatekeeper

Savings angle: "Hi, maybe you can help me — I work with businesses on lowering their card fees. Who's the best person for that, and is now an okay time to reach them?"

Rewards angle: "Hi, maybe you can help me — I help businesses earn points and cash back on the sales they already make. Who's the best person to talk to, and is now an okay time?"

Friendly and brief. Treat the gatekeeper as an ally, not an obstacle.

Follow-up — no response / no-show

Savings angle: "Hi [name] — we hadn't connected, which usually means the timing's off or it slipped down the list. Totally fine either way. Should I close it out, or is it worth a quick look at lowering your fees in [month]? Either answer helps."

Rewards angle: "Hi [name] — never connected, no worries. Quick one: you're running thousands in sales a month and earning nothing back on it. Worth 10 minutes to see what you'd be earning — or should I close it out?"

Negative-reverse re-engage — giving permission to say no often gets a real reply.

Referral introduction

Savings angle: "Hi [name] — [referrer] over at [business] thought we should connect. I helped them [lower their fees / set up their POS], and they figured it'd be worth showing you the same. Open to a quick look?"

Rewards angle: "Hi [name] — [referrer] at [business] thought we should connect. I got them earning points and cash back on their sales, and they figured you'd want the same. Open to a quick look?"

Lead with the referrer's name and result. Warmest intro you'll get — book the time fast.

Inbound lead (they reached out)

Agent: "Hi [name], thanks for reaching out! Quick so I point you right — what made you start looking: the fees, the system, support, or something else? … Got it. Let me ask a couple quick questions and I'll bring the right options."

They're warm — don't over-pitch. Find the trigger, then run discovery.

"Let me think about it" — the save

Agent: "Totally fair. Usually when someone says that it's one of three things — price, timing, or you're just not sure it's worth the switch. Which is closest? I'd rather solve the real concern than chase you."

Isolate the real objection instead of accepting the stall, then handle that one thing.

Text / DM follow-up

Agent: "Hi [name], [your name] from NextPay — you'd asked me to follow up. No pressure; if now's not the time just say so. If it is, I'll keep it to 10 minutes and bring a couple options that fit. [number]"

Short, low-pressure, single ask. Pair with a voicemail or after a missed call.
