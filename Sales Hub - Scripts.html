Scripts & Templates

Word-for-word scripts organized by product, plus ready-to-send text and email templates. Pick a tab, personalize the [brackets], and go. Every script follows the same flow — open, get to the decision maker, ask leading questions, close for the appointment, handle objections.

Opening

Agent: "Hi, this is [your name] with NextPay Business Solutions. Quick question — am I speaking with the owner or the person who handles your credit card processing?"

If yes → proceed. If gatekeeper:

Agent: "Could you connect me with them for just a quick minute? I've got something that could potentially save your business a lot of money."

Once with the decision maker

Agent: "Thanks for your time! The reason I'm calling is simple — credit card fees are probably eating into your profits, right? What if I told you we can eliminate those fees entirely and save you thousands a year? Would that be worth a quick conversation?"

If they show interest:

Agent: "Great! We set businesses up with a cash discount program where the fees shift to the customer. You keep the profit and your processing fees drop to zero. How much are you currently processing in credit cards monthly?"

Leading questions

  • "How much are you currently processing in credit cards monthly?"
  • "Do you know what equipment or system you're using to process payments?"
  • "Would you like a free cost-savings analysis so you can see exactly how much you'd save?"

Close

Agent: "Let's schedule a quick call to go over the details — it'll take no more than 15 minutes. What time works best, later today or tomorrow?"

Objections & responses

"We're not interested."
"I hear you. But if we could show you how to eliminate those fees and save instantly, would you be open to just five minutes to see if it's a fit?"
"We already have a processor."
"Totally get it. But unless your processor is giving you zero fees, it's worth a look — most businesses save thousands with our program."
"It's not a good time."
"No problem. How about I call back when you're less busy? What's a better day — or should we schedule for tomorrow?"

Introduction

Agent: "Good [morning/afternoon], my name is [Your Name] with NextPay Business Solutions, where we specialize in empowering businesses like yours with comprehensive, customized merchant services. May I start by asking what your current setup for payment processing is?"

Identify needs

Listen to their setup & pain points, then:

Agent: "That's very insightful, thank you. It sounds like you're looking to improve [their pain point — fees, speed, customer experience]. Many of our clients faced the same thing, and I'd love to show you how we can make a real difference."

Present the value

  • Advanced technology — the latest POS systems and gateways: fast, reliable, secure.
  • Lower fees — transparent pricing with competitive rates that cut processing costs.
  • Customer support — a dedicated team to resolve any issue quickly.

Benefit: "You don't just save on costs — you improve the checkout experience, which drives higher satisfaction and repeat business."

Handle objections

Agent: "I understand switching providers can feel daunting. Our onboarding is streamlined, and we offer flexible terms with no hidden fees — we make the transition as smooth as possible for you and your team."

Call to action & close

Agent: "What I'd love to do next is schedule a short demo so you can see it in action. How does early next week look? You'll get a firsthand look at how easily it integrates with your current operations."

Close: "Thank you for considering NextPay Business Solutions for your merchant services. We're dedicated to giving you the best solutions and service in the industry — I look forward to our next steps together."
