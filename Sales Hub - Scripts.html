Scripts & Templates

Word-for-word cold-call scripts plus ready-to-send text and email templates. Pick a tab, personalize the brackets, and go.

Opening

Agent: "Hi, this is [your name] with NextPay Business Solutions. Quick question — am I speaking with the owner or the person who handles your credit card processing?"

If yes → proceed. If gatekeeper:

Agent: "Could you connect me with them for just a quick minute? I've got something that could potentially save your business a lot of money."

Once with the decision maker

Agent: "Thanks for your time! The reason I'm calling is simple — credit card fees are probably eating into your profits, right? What if I told you we can eliminate those fees entirely and save you thousands a year? Would that be worth a quick chat?"

If they show interest:

Agent: "Great! We set businesses up with a cash discount program where the fees shift to the customer. You keep the profit and your processing fees drop to zero. How much are you currently processing in credit cards monthly?"

Leading questions

  • "How much are you currently processing in credit cards monthly?"
  • "Do you know what equipment or system you're using to process payments?"
  • "Would you like a free cost-savings analysis so you can see exactly how much you'd save?"

Close

Agent: "Let's schedule a quick call to go over the details — it'll take no more than 15 minutes. What time works best, later today or tomorrow?"

Objections & responses

"We're not interested."
"I hear you. But if we could show you how to eliminate those fees and save instantly, would you be open to just five minutes to see if it's a fit?"
"We already have a processor."
"Totally get it. But unless your processor is giving you zero fees, it's worth a look — most businesses save thousands with our program."
"It's not a good time."
"No problem. How about I call back when you're less busy? What's a better day — or should we schedule for tomorrow?"
