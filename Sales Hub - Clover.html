Deal info — common questions

Quick answers to have ready for the merchant conversation.

Are there any true upfront costs for the merchant?

Shipping is the only cost not covered — and it can be covered by you (the agent), the merchant, or split. Everything else for the hardware is included in the monthly placement fee.

Is there an installation fee?

No installation fee. It's all done by phone. Everything comes plug-and-play, and if the merchant has questions or needs help, they have access to our Implementation team to assist.

Is there a training cost?

No training cost. It's done by the placement team over the phone.

Is there a contract length?

No contract. You can add an Early Termination Fee if you choose, but it's not required either.

For repairs / replacements, how is the order handled?

Handled by our Tech team. We troubleshoot the device, and if it's deemed no longer working we send a replacement. The broken device usually does not need to be returned — we just have to submit proof that it's no longer working.

What is the underwriting process for Clover? What documents are required?

Driver's license and a copy of a voided check or a signed bank letter are the only requirements. Underwriting goes pretty quickly if there are no pending items.

How long does the merchant pay this monthly cost?

For the lifetime of the account — there is currently no buyout option. But the placement fee includes unlimited replacements, no contract, and no restocking fees (Clover charges 20%).

Add-ons & software costs — what's extra?

Dual Pricing: yes, there's a monthly cost — it needs a Marketplace app, $9.95–$19.95/mo depending on which app the merchant chooses.  Online Ordering: no monthly cost.  Loyalty: Clover's basic version is included at no charge; the more in-depth upgraded version is $99/month.
