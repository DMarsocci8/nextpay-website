What does a POS system actually cost in 2026?

Real numbers across every pricing model — monthly software, hardware, placement programs and the processing that hides underneath.

"How much is a POS?" has four answers — software, hardware, setup and processing — and vendors love quoting whichever one sounds cheapest. Here are the real 2026 numbers across the systems we install, all four layers included.

Software: $0 to ~$99 per month

  • Square: free tier is real; the Plus plan restaurants actually use is $49/mo
  • SkyTab: $29.99/mo per station — restaurant-grade, hardware included
  • PAYS: all-inclusive $59 / $79 / $99 tiers with 0% dual pricing built in
  • Korona: $59–$99 per register, processor-agnostic
  • Quantic: $60 first station / $50 each additional (Enterprise $90/$80)

Hardware: $59 to ~$2,600 — or $0 down

  • Entry: Square Reader $59, SumUp Solo $54
  • Mid: Square Terminal $299, SumUp Terminal $249, Clover Flex $758
  • Full stations: Square Register $899, Quantic bundles from $919, Clover Station Duo ~$2,078, Korona bundles $1,400–$2,600
  • Or skip upfront entirely: SkyTab and Clover placement put hardware on the counter for $0 down — placement programs compared here

Setup: the layer nobody quotes

Menu builds, inventory imports, training, network gear. Some vendors charge four figures; our installs bundle setup with the system, and hardware ships programmed. Always ask — "free POS" quotes often earn it back here.

Processing: the layer that dwarfs the rest

A $49/month software fee is $588/year. A 2.8% effective rate on $40k/month is $13,400/year. The processing model — flat rate vs. interchange-plus vs. 0% dual pricing — moves more money than every other line combined. Price the POS and the processing together or you're not really pricing either.

Realistic all-in examples: food truck on a handheld — under $100/month all-in with dual pricing. Single-location restaurant, two SkyTab stations + KDS — ~$90/month software, $0 hardware, 0% processing. Retail shop owning a Korona bundle — ~$2,300 once + $79/month, processing quoted at match-or-beat.
