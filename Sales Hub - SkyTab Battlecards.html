SkyTab by Shift4 — Battlecards

Sell SkyTab by Shift4 against the restaurant systems you'll run into — who they are, where they're strong, where they fall short, and exactly how SkyTab wins. Straight from Shift4's official sales battle cards.

vs Toast vs Clover vs Square vs SpotOn vs Lightspeed vs Heartland vs Revel vs Tabit

SkyTab's recurring edge on every card: end-to-end Shift4 payments means loyalty, online ordering, QR Order & Pay, marketing and Marketplace integrations come free (no setup/monthly add-on fees); hardware is included with the POS; true offline mode; the Advantage / dual-pricing program takes processing toward zero; and a mature reseller network plus a direct SkyTab team for real local install, training and support.

SkyTab vs

Toast

Who are they? A restaurant-first cloud POS that grew fast on VC funding, a 2021 IPO, a direct sales team and aggressive marketing. Proprietary Android hardware + software with built-in processing, aimed at more complex restaurants.

Their products & pricing

Order & Pay, Gift Cards, Partner Connect, third-party delivery bundle, xtraCHEF inventory, Marketing + Loyalty, Payroll & Team. Hardware: Toast Flex, Go 2, Kiosk. Setup + monthly + processing, and every add-on module is monetized separately — costly for a fully-integrated merchant.

Where Toast is strong

  • Mature cloud product; software handles restaurant intricacies (modifiers, etc.)
  • Fast development & iteration; big dev team
  • "Sticky" model — hooks merchants with initial POS-and-go
  • IPO cash fuels sales teams; strong brand awareness & marketing

Where Toast falls short

  • Monthly third-party integration fees inflate total cost
  • Base sold with SaaS + processing; add-on modules each cost extra
  • Direct-sales model = inefficient, mostly-remote install & support
How SkyTab wins
  • End-to-end payments open up a plethora of features free of charge — setup and monthly — with contactless & online ordering built in
  • Own processing: rates by card type (often less than a flat rate), more control, less downtime exposure
  • Marketplace + enhanced Lighthouse Business Manager included at no extra charge — pay only for partner services
  • Mature reseller community with nationwide local install/train/support, plus a direct SkyTab sales team

SkyTab vs

Clover

Who are they? A cloud POS software, hardware and processing company owned by Fiserv (Fortune 500). A one-size-fits-all product used by restaurants, retail, salons and more.

Their products & pricing

Counter Service & Table Service plans; KDS; free Clover Online Ordering; BentoBox. Hardware: Station, Flex, Mini. $60–80/mo + $9.95 per additional device.

Where Clover is strong

  • Great hardware and reliable payments; strong brand recognition
  • Owned by Fiserv; low rates depending on the sales channel
  • Low software price relative to Toast; extensive reseller network

Where Clover falls short

  • Not mature or tailored for sophisticated restaurants
  • App-market model drives high total price; expensive hardware (channel-dependent)
  • Highly variable processing rates; minimal innovation
How SkyTab wins
  • SkyTab hardware is more affordable and as good (or better) a form factor — and it's owned by Shift4 (about a third of North American restaurants use Shift4)
  • Purpose-built for restaurants and more feature-rich; Clover is one-size-fits-all and slow to innovate in the restaurant space
  • Lower cost of ownership over the long run, especially with hardware included
  • Clover's "low price" hides paid app-market add-ons — and watch for high reseller rate schedules

SkyTab vs

Square

Who are they? A mobile-payments company with POS, hardware and SMB software. A comprehensive free base POS and no fixed monthly cost make for a very low barrier to entry; origins in mobile card readers built the brand.

Their products & pricing

Square Stand/iPad, Terminal, Online site, Payments, Team Management, KDS, Payroll, Loyalty, Marketing. Free: $0 (2.5% + 10¢) · Plus: $60/mo (2.9% + 30¢) · Premium: contracted.

Where Square is strong

  • Free entry-level option — very low commitment, grow into paid later
  • Very easy to set up and use; minimal hardware
  • Strong brand awareness backed by traditional advertising

Where Square falls short

  • Not practical for multi-channel / multi-location — lacks bar & restaurant depth (modifiers, channels)
  • Built for iOS; limited hardware options (e.g. KDS)
  • Relies on remote self-install; less live/on-site support
How SkyTab wins
  • End-to-end payments — features, hardware and functionality free of charge; more focused on processing than locking you to software
  • Built for Food & Beverage: modifiers, integrations, multi-location so a growing merchant never has to switch POS
  • Mature reseller network with local install/train/support, plus chat and phone
  • Android hardware options; Shift4 brand lift (SpaceX, St. Jude); direct SkyTab team

SkyTab vs

SpotOn

Who are they? A mix-and-match restaurant POS covering the basics (sales/inventory, analytics, online ordering), with add-ons like loyalty and reservations available for an extra charge.

Their products & pricing

SpotOn Restaurant, Poynt, Delivery (DoorDash), Serve Handheld, Connect, CDS/KDS. POS w/ processing $25/mo · w/o processing $195/mo · Restaurant $65/mo + hardware · Poynt $15/mo + setup · Loyalty $65/mo · Website $65/mo · processing 1.99% + 20¢.

Where SpotOn is strong

  • Easy restaurant-specific platform — detailed modifiers, menu tools, web-based access anywhere
  • Marketing/business support (website builder, loyalty, wide reporting); 24/7 chat, phone, email
  • Low processing (1.99% + 20¢) — good for high-volume merchants

Where SpotOn falls short

  • Pushes its own touchscreen hardware with a monthly fee
  • No standard pricing — a quote is required to know true cost
  • Add-on fees (loyalty, etc.) stack up; processing is expensive if not on their payments (2.99% + 20¢)
How SkyTab wins
  • Lighthouse Business Manager (BI, marketing, reputation, customer engagement, loyalty) + Marketplace, with free SkyTab Online & SkyTab QR
  • Competitive end-to-end payments — loyalty, email marketing, reputation, social & third-party integrations are free (setup and monthly)
  • Android hardware free with POS service; straightforward monthly costs
  • Custom enterprise agreements available

SkyTab vs

Lightspeed

Who are they? A Canadian cloud company spanning POS, eCommerce, payments, accounting and multi-location management, serving the US and worldwide; publicly traded (NYSE/TSX).

Their products & pricing

Restaurant POS (L-Series), Delivery (Deliverect), Payments, Customer-Facing Display, Self-Order Menu, Kitchen, Loyalty, Order Ahead, Accounting. Non-proprietary hardware. Essentials $29/mo · Plus $119/mo · Pro $289/mo · Enterprise contracted.

Where Lightspeed is strong

  • Diversified portfolio (retail, restaurant, golf/resort, eComm) — resilient across markets
  • Owns Lightspeed Payments plus three other processing options — flexibility to win/keep business
  • Flexible, non-proprietary hardware (reuse prior gear); public-company M&A muscle

Where Lightspeed falls short

  • Steep learning curve — lots of config, add-ons and integrations to set up and train
  • Add-on pricing means a custom quote is needed to understand true cost
  • Offline needs "LiteServer" — and still can't run credit/debit/gift cards or third-party integrations while offline
How SkyTab wins
  • Diverse portfolio with ongoing US bar & restaurant investment driven by merchant feedback
  • End-to-end payments — features and functionality free of charge, setup and monthly
  • Straightforward, predictable service-based offering (dealer-friendly); Marketplace + Lighthouse included free
  • True offline mode — devices auto-switch on connection failure and the queue processes when back online

SkyTab vs

Heartland

Who are they? A subsidiary of Global Payments; acquired MobileBytes (now Heartland Restaurant POS). Mostly dealer-exclusive, with some direct sales via CSMs in markets without strong dealer presence.

Their products & pricing

Processing, gift/eGift, email marketing, customer display, kiosk, loyalty/ordering app, KDS. Priced by terminal + monthly software; $75/mo mobile app; SaaS-plus-upfront or PaaS (one monthly rate).

Where Heartland is strong

  • Cheaper when POS + payments are bundled; good eGift, free online ordering, native kiosk
  • Easy setup; skip-the-line & tableside QR ordering; loyalty history
  • Good for small/quick-serve; mobile EMV tableside; free open-API integrations

Where Heartland falls short

  • iOS/iCloud reliance causes problems; not commercial-grade hardware; KDS needs iPad, no remote control
  • Critical features unavailable if not processing with Heartland (pre-auth, offline, SMS, cash discount); no KDS reporting
  • "Support is a nightmare"; hidden/junk fees, EMV Failover Fee, $125/mo Compliance Fee; surcharge program works poorly
  • Heavy dealer reliance — inconsistent pricing, install and support quality
How SkyTab wins
  • Be up-front and clear about payments — with Shift4 they're fully integrated (no separate mobile payment app), with easy Marketplace integrations
  • Shift4's Advantage Program is a better, more compliant option: dual pricing, supplemental fee, and cash discount
  • Advanced KDS features (rush, merge, list view, all-day production) with KDS reporting on the roadmap
  • SkyTab QR Order & Pay, growing gift/loyalty/kiosk, and a bigger enterprise focus

SkyTab vs

Revel

Who are they? An iPad-based POS and cloud pioneer with expansive hospitality features. Since 2018 it focuses on multi-location QSR and no longer targets single-location merchants — a fit for established multi-location operators.

Their products & pricing

POS, open API, Loyalty, Kitchen Management, Online Ordering, Delivery, Flexible Payments, Kiosk. Essentials/Enterprise contracted; Revel Advantage flat-rate processing; POS terminal $99/mo per terminal (2-terminal minimum, 3-year contract); implementation from $674.

Where Revel is strong

  • Enterprise management — deep configuration, scales with the merchant, many integrations
  • Flexible for various merchant types with targeted features
  • Multi-channel (drive-thru, delivery, online), order/delivery tracking, customer text updates

Where Revel falls short

  • High cost with custom quotes; best pricing needs a 3-year agreement + 2-terminal minimum
  • Everything is SaaS (KDS, CDS, online ordering) — hundreds of dollars in monthly fees
  • iOS only; printers/accessories vary
How SkyTab wins
  • Grow-with-the-merchant SkyTab ecosystem (Mobile, Online, Glass, CDS, KDS) with continuous improvement
  • End-to-end payments — features free of charge, setup and monthly
  • Service pricing model focused on payments — end the contract as needed, custom enterprise agreements
  • Android hardware options

SkyTab vs

Tabit

Who are they? An Israeli-founded, mobile-first iPad POS (now HQ'd in Aventura, FL). Payment-agnostic with no direct sales — it grows by recruiting resellers who keep their payments revenue.

Their products & pricing

TabitPAD (POS), TabitGuest (reservations), TabitOrder (online ordering), TabitInsight (BI, not real-time), TabitChef (mobile reporting), TabitWheels (delivery), TabitKitchen (KDS). Variable dealer-to-dealer pricing, generally ~$49/mo per mobile device.

Where Tabit is strong

  • Mobile-first — every device is a full POS station; faster comms to the kitchen
  • Cloud-based centralized database links online ordering, reservations and more
  • Reseller-focused with aggressive partner programs; rich native ecosystem

Where Tabit falls short

  • No offline mode yet — recommends a redundant WAN (extra cost) or merchants can't process
  • Inconsistent experience — dealer-dependent, no remote support
  • Clunky, complex back-end setup; partners need heavy training
How SkyTab wins
  • iPads are costly and Wi-Fi can be unreliable — the most likely downtime is at high volume; SkyTab is cloud-based with a robust ecosystem including payments
  • SkyTab offline mode — merchants can still process credit card payments during an outage
  • Native solutions plus Marketplace integrations; intuitive back office, most config from the POS itself
  • Robust support — Shift4 or resellers can remote into terminals; plus a direct SkyTab team
