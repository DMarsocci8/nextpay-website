Why Square works

Versatile & simple

  • Versatile use cases: retail, restaurants, professional services, beauty, food trucks & more.
  • Simple onboarding: merchant signs up online — no complex underwriting for most businesses.
  • Two pricing programs: interchange-plus (rack rate) OR 4% flat-rate dual pricing — agents pick what fits.
  • Co-branded 4% program: Square + Solutions in Payments created a joint 4% flat-rate signup link.
  • Ecommerce built in: online store, invoicing & payment links included.

Two links. Two programs. Know the difference.

Option 1 · 4% Flat Rate — Dual Pricing

Cash Discount OR Service Charge

Merchant passes the 4% processing fee to the customer. Two options:

  • Cash Discount: offer a discount to cash-paying customers to offset processing.
  • Service Charge: add 4% to all card transactions to offset processing fees.
4% Dual Pricing signup ↗
Option 2 · Interchange Plus (Rack Rate)

Standard IC+ Pricing

Merchant pays interchange-plus (rack rate) processing — the merchant absorbs all processing costs.

  • Welcome email arrives after signup
  • Welcome email contains the rack-rate application link
  • Walk merchant through SaaS fee & equipment selection
