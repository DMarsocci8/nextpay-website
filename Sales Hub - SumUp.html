The pitch angle

Where SumUp wins the deal.

Against Square

Same simplicity and flat-rate feel, but the full-POS monthly total comes in far lower (SumUp's own math: ~$218/mo vs ~$463) — and the merchant gets install, training and a real rep through us instead of a help center.

For micro-merchants & mobile businesses

A $54 reader or the $249 Terminal with $0/mo software is the lowest barrier to entry we sell. Great door-opener for food trucks, market vendors, mobile services — and they can grow into the full SumUp POS later.

Know when to switch products

SumUp is flat rate only. If the merchant's #1 pain is processing fees, a dual-pricing setup (DejaPay Pro, Quantic, Dejavoo terminal programs) gets them to 0% — SumUp can't. Match the product to the pain.
