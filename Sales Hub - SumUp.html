The pitch angle

Where SumUp wins the deal.

Against Square

Same simplicity and flat-rate feel, but the full-POS monthly total comes in far lower (SumUp's own math: ~$218/mo vs ~$463) — and the merchant gets install, training and a real rep through us instead of a help center.

For micro-merchants & mobile businesses

A $54 reader or the $249 Terminal with $0/mo software is the lowest barrier to entry we sell. Great door-opener for food trucks, market vendors, mobile services — and they can grow into the full SumUp POS later.

Match the program to the pain

SumUp now includes a dual-pricing program, so fee-sensitive merchants can go to near-zero processing on SumUp itself. If they need a different form factor (a standalone terminal or gateway), a DejaPay Pro / Quantic / Dejavoo dual-pricing setup is still on the table. Match the product to the pain.

SumUp Connect — loyalty & marketing

The growth engine that brings customers back — built in, not a bolt-on. SumUp reports return visits up to +200% and second visits +45%.

Identify every customer

Captures name, email & birthday at checkout. Customers sign up with just a phone number — no app required. Loyal Connect users spend 10× more over time.

Automated rewards & win-back

Behavior-based messages welcome new customers and win back ~10% of lost ones. AutoPilot nudges customers absent 15/30/60+ days — bringing them back 2–3× more often.

Promotions & segmentation

One-off promos to everyone or a segment lift visits ~70%. Build custom audience groups, fire SMS/push campaigns, and track it all live in the merchant dashboard.

Connect is included on Connect Plus ($199/mo) and Connect Pro ($289/mo) — Plus adds the rewards program + 2 monthly campaigns, Pro adds daily AI-driven promos, Google review boost and unlimited campaigns.

By industry — who to target & how to pitch

SumUp's sweet spots, the lead talk track, and who to steer away from.

Quick-service restaurants

Target: beverage specialists (boba, coffee, tea, smoothie), bakeries & dessert shops, fast casual, Spanish-speaking owners, current Clover & Square users.

Avoid: full-service/sit-down, Herbalife/nutrition, food halls & ghost kitchens, seasonal, Toast users.

Lead with: modifiers & multi-menu, a self-service kiosk (orders 25–35% larger, wait −50%), dual pricing, and Urban Piper delivery (Uber Eats, DoorDash, Grubhub & more) with no double entry.

Retail

Target: vape/tobacco, pet, boutique, specialized grocery, hobby/toy, beauty supply, wine & liquor.

Avoid: thrift/consignment, cannabis, firearms, adult.

Lead with: Master Product Editor & Product Matrix for big SKU counts, real-time low-stock alerts, in-store + online catalog sync, "Nearby" (appear on Connect screens at nearby shops), and SMS autopilot (2–3× visits).

Services

Target: nail & hair salons, spas, barber shops, tattoo shops, auto service centers.

Avoid: dry cleaners.

Lead with: integrated online booking, layered appointment reminders & deposits, staff schedules & commission tracking, Review Boost, and SMS autopilot — second visits +45%.

Vape & tobacco / age-restricted

Target: vape, CBD and smoke shops.

Lead with: SumUp is the leading platform that lets age-restricted shops send compliant SMS & push, plus low-stock alerts and visit tracking. Visits climb +175% after 60/90/120 days of no visit.
