Why food trucks are different

Four constraints a curbside business lives with

We tuned the recommendations below around the realities of running a kitchen on wheels.

Mobile & outdoor

Hardware has to be handheld, battery-powered, and tough enough for service in the sun, rain, and a moving truck.

Spotty connectivity

Festival fields and food-truck parks have weak signal. You need offline mode that queues sales and syncs later.

Fast lines & tips

A 20-minute lunch rush means checkout in seconds with a clean tip prompt that actually lifts your average ticket.

Thin margins

Every basis point matters. Flat, transparent processing — and we'll match or beat your current rate.
