Product Knowledge

A quick sell-sheet for every product — who it's best for, the key features, and the talk-track to pitch it. Skim before a demo so you always know what you're selling and why it fits.

How to use this: match the business to the right product, then lead with the talk-track. Need a deeper spec sheet or pricing? See the Product Catalog and Pricing center. These sell-sheets also feed the personalized one-pagers in Marketing Assets.

POS

Square

Best for: retail, services & smaller restaurants that want a familiar, easy-to-run system.

  • Fast to set up — staff already know how to use it
  • Full hardware range — Register, Stand, Terminal & Reader
  • Built-in online store, invoices & e-commerce
  • Free plan to start; add payroll, loyalty & marketing

Talk-track: "The system everyone already knows — placed by us with better pricing so more of every sale stays with you."
