Common questions

Do I have to change anything about how I accept payments?
Nothing changes. You process payments exactly as you do today — points are earned and tracked in the background automatically.
Is this only available if I process with NextPay?
Yes — the rewards program is a benefit included with your NextPay merchant account. If you're not yet a NextPay customer, contact us and we'll get you set up.
When do my points show up?
Points are calculated based on your monthly processing volume and credited to your rewards account regularly. You can check your balance anytime through your portal.
Can I use the rewards for my employees or team?
Absolutely. Your points are yours to use however you'd like — for yourself, your family, your team, or even as employee recognition rewards.
How do I redeem?
Once you're enrolled, you'll have access to your rewards portal where you can browse the full catalog and redeem points directly. No phone calls needed.
