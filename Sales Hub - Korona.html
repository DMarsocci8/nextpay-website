Ordering rules (from the dealer sheet)

All sales are final (no returns or exchanges) · allow 10–15 business days for delivery · units ship preconfigured for sales functionality only — POS terminals can't create products, don't access KORONA back office, and don't include WiFi. Merchant needs back-office access or has a special use case? Contact the KORONA rep before ordering.
