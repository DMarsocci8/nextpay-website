Marketing & Brand

Marketing to Your Customer

Marketing involves understanding customer needs (e.g., safe, fun indoor play) and delivering messages that address them through appropriate channels — social media, email, ads, on-site signage. The goal is not just awareness but conversions: turning interest into visits, bookings, or repeat business while building long-term relationships.

Training session · 20 Feb '26

Basic Steps for Effective Marketing

  1. Know Your Customer — use the Target Audience and Personas sections to focus efforts on parents of young children seeking clean, developmental play.
  2. Craft Clear Messages — highlight unique benefits (largest indoor sandbox, construction theme, cleanliness, no reservations) using approved taglines and tone.
  3. Choose Channels Wisely — leverage provided tools: the local Facebook page for community building, optional emails for promotions, and portal-ordered materials for visibility.
  4. Measure and Adjust — track simple metrics (e.g., post engagement, visitor feedback). Refine based on what works.
  5. Stay On-Brand — always adhere to guidelines for consistency and professionalism.
🎯Selling points to lead with: largest indoor sandbox · construction theme · cleanliness · no reservations needed.
Marketing & Brand

Target Audience

Understanding the target audience ensures all marketing efforts are directed toward families most likely to visit, engage, and convert into repeat customers — optimizing resource allocation, enhancing messaging relevance, and driving high conversion rates by addressing specific needs and preferences.

Core Customers

Parents and caregivers of children ages 7 and under, particularly toddlers and preschoolers (ages 1–5). These families seek safe, clean, screen-free indoor activities that promote developmental play — especially during inclement weather, weekdays, or for social outings.

Key Characteristics

  • Reside within a 5–10 mile radius of the location (local trade area)
  • Middle to upper-middle income households capable of affording drop-in play sessions or birthday parties
  • Value cleanliness, safety, and low-stimulation environments over chaotic, overcrowded venues
  • Prioritize experiences that support child development (motor skills, creativity, problem-solving) while allowing parental relaxation (e.g., comfortable seating, Wi-Fi, coffee)

Secondary Target Audience

  • Grandparents or extended family members accompanying children
  • Playdate groups, mommy-and-me classes, or parent networks
  • Organizers of birthday parties or private events seeking unique, hassle-free venues
  • Daycares, early learning centers, and kindergarten groups that want a hands-on field trip

Speaking About the Business

Every interaction is an opportunity to subtly advertise the company and your specific location — so be intentional in how you reference it. How the sandbox is described impacts how customers perceive the business and brand: emphasize positive, constructive language and call out key details as openly as possible. The more specific you are about the facilities, the easier it is to create brand awareness that stands out from competitors.

Training session · 12 Feb '26

Naming the Business

✔ Acceptable

  • Little Diggers
  • Little Diggers [Specific Location]

✖ Not Acceptable

  • "Diggers" — too generic, does not reinforce the brand's identity or name
  • "The Sandbox" — too generic, does not provide any specific details about the brand

Why it matters: the business name is the brand. When customers become confused about who we are:

  • They get frustrated when they can't find us or go to the wrong place
  • They leave reviews that do not reflect our business or services
  • They tell their friends not to come in to play

Be Specific — Language Swaps

Instead of "large, indoor sandbox"

"Over 1000 sq. ft. of indoor sandbox space and 200+ toys, trucks, and excavators."

Instead of "digger toys"

"An assortment of toys, trucks, and excavators, including ride-on excavators, classic dump trucks, and the Sand Stacker."

Instead of "sand"

"Nontoxic, silica-free sand that is renewably sourced from the Bahamas."

Talking About Cleaning — Protect the Proprietary Methods

🤫We want people to know we take cleaning seriously, but our proprietary cleaning methods separate us from competitors — avoid sharing the specifics when asked. Use these authentic, honest lines instead:

Daily cleaning

We have rigorous protocols to disinfect the sand and work with it daily to maintain a clean environment.

If a child has an accident

We have a rigorous protocol in place to isolate, excavate, and sanitize the sandbox if a child has an accident.

🚫 Terms We Avoid

  • "Sand pit" — we brand our facilities as sandboxes and avoid the term "pit"
  • "Litter box" — avoid any references to cats, cat urine, and litter boxes when speaking about the sandbox. Likewise, delete any comments on social posts with those references.

Social Media Content Policy

To ensure all social content builds trust, protects the brand, and drives real-world visits and bookings.

Scope: all employees, contractors, volunteers & third parties representing Little Diggers or a franchise
📌Content must utilize approved templates and assets obtained through the dedicated chat or the ordering portal.

Posting Expectations

  • 2–5 posts per week recommended
  • Quality > quantity

✔ Prioritize

  • Real, in-playspace content (or content from events) whenever possible
  • Children at play, staff interactions, and clean environments
  • Use provided caption frameworks and approved messaging

✖ Avoid

  • Over-editing
  • Overly sales-oriented language
  • Sarcasm or edgy humor
  • Medical, developmental, or safety claims
  • Negativity regarding customers, kids, parenting choices, etc.
  • Explicit language or music
  • Trends that conflict with parent trust or safety perception

🚫 Prohibited Practices

  • Political messaging
  • Unauthorized vendor use for promotions
  • Exaggerated claims
  • Competitor comparisons or disparagement
  • Misleading offers

Social Media Account Policy

Social media is a primary discovery, trust-building, and conversion channel for Little Diggers. The goal: a consistent, high-trust brand nationally, while local locations connect authentically with their community.

Scope: all employees, contractors, volunteers & third parties representing Little Diggers or a franchise

All Little Diggers franchisees and corporate stores are provided with a dedicated Facebook page. Social media usage must promote the brand's services, engage customers positively, and align with the core vision of physical play in a digital world. Personal opinions, off-brand promotions, or content potentially harmful to the brand's reputation are strictly prohibited. All activity must comply with platform terms of service and applicable regulations, including FTC guidelines on endorsements and disclosures.

Account Ownership

Corporate Owns

  • National Facebook, Instagram, TikTok accounts

Franchisee Owns

  • One official Facebook Page per location (access provisioned by Corporate)

🚫 Prohibited Actions

  • Locations may not create additional social accounts on any platform without written approval
  • Instagram and TikTok are managed nationally unless otherwise approved
  • Copying, downloading, or removing trade secret materials without authorization
Marketing & Brand

Content Creation Policy

All content created, posted, shared, or distributed by franchisees should reflect the core values of the Little Diggers brand.

Scope: franchisees, their employees, contractors & representatives

🚫 Prohibited Content — never create, post, share, or allow:

  • Explicit, suggestive, sexual, violent, profane, or adult-oriented language, imagery, or themes
  • Disparaging, defamatory, derogatory, mocking, or negative remarks about any individual, group, competitor, customer, vendor, employee, or other entity
  • Content that promotes, glorifies, or depicts illegal activities, substance abuse, discrimination, harassment, bullying, or hate speech
  • Political statements, endorsements, partisan commentary, or references to controversial social, religious, or cultural issues
  • Content that could reasonably be perceived as insensitive, offensive, or inappropriate for children or families
  • Negative reviews, complaints, or criticisms of Little Diggers (corporate or franchised locations), other franchisees, or brand partners — even if intended humorously
  • Material depicting unsafe behavior, disregard for safety rules, or conditions contrary to the brand's emphasis on a safe play environment
  • Content featuring identifiable guests (including children) without proper consent per the Photo & Video Release Policy, or that violates any opt-out designation

✔ Required Standards — all content must:

  • Be positive, uplifting, engaging, and appropriate for a family audience
  • Accurately represent the Little Diggers experience, facilities, and services
  • Use only high-quality, professional-looking imagery and language
  • Comply with all applicable copyright, trademark, and intellectual property laws
  • Adhere to platform-specific guidelines (e.g., social media community standards)
  • Include appropriate disclaimers when required (e.g., "Results may vary" for promotional offers)
  • Be reviewed and approved by designated franchise location management prior to publication, where practical

Franchisee Obligations

  • Train all staff involved in content creation on this policy and brand guidelines
  • Monitor social media accounts, websites, and other channels under their control for compliance
  • Promptly remove or correct non-compliant content upon discovery or Franchisor direction
  • Report suspected violations by employees or third parties to location management and, when appropriate, to the Franchisor

Compliance & Enforcement

Compliance with this policy is a material term of the Franchise Agreement. Violations — including the creation, publication, or failure to remove prohibited content — may result in remedial action, up to and including termination of the franchise agreement. Franchisees must cooperate fully with any Franchisor request to remove or modify content. Direct suspected violations or content questions immediately to the Franchisor's designated compliance or marketing contact.

Marketing & Brand

Brand & Logo Usage Policy

Standards for the appropriate and consistent application of the Little Diggers brand across all franchise locations and marketing initiatives — safeguarding brand integrity, protecting intellectual property rights, and promoting uniformity in visual presentation and messaging.

Scope: all franchisees, employees, vendors & partners engaged in marketing activities

Governing Document

All brand elements — logo, trademarks, color palette, typography, tagline, imagery guidelines, and visual standards — are defined in the official Little Diggers Brand Design Guidelines (the "Brand Guidelines"), the authoritative reference for proper brand representation.

🗂️The most current version of the Brand Guidelines lives in the franchise portal. Franchisees are responsible for using only the latest approved files and specifications.

Usage Guidelines

  • Brand elements must be applied in strict accordance with the Brand Guidelines.
  • Approved uses: signage, websites, social media platforms, promotional materials, uniforms, and merchandise — provided they align with the family-friendly and educational positioning of Little Diggers.
  • All materials must maintain proper proportions, clear space requirements, minimum sizes, and reproduction quality.
  • Use the pre-approved portal templates for common marketing items: flyers, social media graphics, and advertisements.

🚫 Prohibited Uses

  • Any modification, distortion, recoloring, or unauthorized combination of brand elements
  • Use in contexts that could harm the brand's reputation, including materials promoting violence, discrimination, political, religious, or controversial subjects
  • Reproduction on unofficial merchandise or in any commercial activity without express franchisor approval
  • Unauthorized sublicensing, transfer, or sharing of brand assets with third parties

Approval Process

  • With the exception of social media posts, all marketing materials incorporating Little Diggers brand elements must be submitted for review through the franchise portal at least 14 days prior to production or distribution.
  • The corporate marketing team reviews submissions and provides feedback within 3 business days.
  • Questions on interpreting the Brand Guidelines go to the corporate marketing team.

Compliance & Enforcement

  • Franchisees are responsible for compliance by their staff, agencies, and vendors.
  • Annual brand usage training is provided as part of ongoing support.
  • Non-compliance may result in required corrective actions, withdrawal of materials, or penalties per the Franchise Agreement.
  • Little Diggers retains the right to audit marketing materials and brand usage at any time.

Trademark Policy

Standards governing the use, protection, and enforcement of the Little Diggers trademarks — a core component of the franchise system's intellectual property. Goal: preserve the marks' legal validity and strength, prevent dilution or infringement, and ensure consistent usage per federal trademark law and the Franchise Agreement.

Scope: all franchisees, employees, vendors & partners

Governing Documents

The Little Diggers trademarks — registered marks, service marks, logos, and associated designations — are defined in the Brand Design Guidelines and the Franchise Agreement (ownership, registration status, permitted scope of use). Always refer to the most current versions in the franchise portal.

⚖️The franchisor retains exclusive ownership of all trademarks — no rights transfer beyond the limited license in the Franchise Agreement.

Rights & License Grant

The Franchise Agreement grants a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable license to use the trademarks solely in connection with operating an authorized indoor sandbox playspace location, in strict accordance with approved guidelines. The license is conditioned on ongoing compliance with quality standards, operational requirements, and brand specifications.

Usage Requirements

  • Use trademarks exactly as specified in the Brand Design Guidelines — no alteration, modification, or dilution.
  • Apply proper notices consistently: ™ for unregistered marks, ® for registered marks.
  • Notify the franchisor immediately of any suspected infringement, unauthorized use, or third-party claims to similar marks.
  • The franchisor reserves the right to police and enforce trademark rights, including legal action against infringers, at its sole discretion.

🚫 Prohibited Uses

  • Unauthorized reproduction, adaptation, or combination of trademarks with other elements
  • Use that could cause confusion, dilution, or damage to the brand's reputation — including political, discriminatory, or controversial contexts
  • Registering (or attempting to register) similar marks, domain names, or trade names without franchisor consent
  • Continued use after termination or expiration of the Franchise Agreement

Protection & Enforcement

  • The franchisor maintains federal trademark registrations where applicable and monitors for infringement.
  • Franchisees must cooperate in enforcement efforts — providing evidence or ceasing use as directed.
  • Third-party claims against a franchisee's use are handled per the Franchise Agreement (may include defense obligations or indemnification per the Franchise Disclosure Document).
  • No independent legal action related to the trademarks without prior written approval.

Approval & Monitoring

  • All proposed trademark uses in marketing, signage, or other applications follow the approval process in the Brand & Logo Usage Policy (above).
  • The franchisor may inspect locations, materials, and operations to verify compliance.
  • Non-compliance may bring corrective directives, penalties under the Franchise Agreement, or termination of the license and franchise rights.

Donations & Gifts Policy

Which organizations and initiatives are eligible for Little Diggers donations or in-kind support — keeping contributions aligned with the mission of encouraging children to thrive and engage with the physical world around them.

✔ Supported Initiatives

  • Foster Care & Adoption — organizations that support or work with foster care and adoption in the local community
  • Infant & Pregnancy Loss Organizations — groups providing support and care to families who have lost an infant
  • Child-Focused Programs — direct support for early childhood education, developmental play, health, or wellness (e.g., preschools, daycares, pediatric therapy programs, children with cancer, etc.)
  • School and PTA/PTO Fundraisers — auctions, fun runs, or events at elementary schools, particularly those serving families with children ages 7 and under
  • Local Youth Organizations — non-profits focused on children (e.g., children's museums, libraries' kids programs, family resource centers)
  • Community Events for Families — festivals, fairs, or gatherings emphasizing child-friendly activities in the location's territory

✖ Declined Initiatives

  • Political campaigns, candidates, or advocacy groups
  • Adult-focused causes or events without clear child involvement
  • Animal/pet-related charities or events
  • Organizations outside the local community or with national/international scope (referred to corporate if appropriate)
  • Requests promoting competing businesses or conflicting values

Third Party Endorsement Policy

Little Diggers requires strict brand neutrality — no use of the brand that could reasonably create the perception of endorsement, political or otherwise, by third parties (customers, vendors, community organizations, influencers, charities, sponsors, or other external entities).

Franchisees must ensure that no third party is permitted to imply, suggest, or appear to suggest that Little Diggers, the brand, or any franchise location endorses, supports, sponsors, is affiliated with, or approves of their products, services, candidates, political parties, causes, ballot measures, or any other position, organization, or activity.

🚫 Prohibited Conduct — franchisees shall not permit, facilitate, or allow any third party to:

  • Use the Little Diggers name, logo, trademarks, trade dress, photographs of franchise locations, uniforms, signage, vehicles, or any other brand identifiers in connection with any endorsement, sponsorship, affiliation, or support statement — political, commercial, charitable, or otherwise
  • State or imply that Little Diggers or any franchise location endorses, sponsors, recommends, partners with, or approves of:
    • Any political candidate, political party, elected official, political organization, ballot initiative, referendum, or partisan issue
    • Any non-political third-party product, service, cause, organization, event, individual, or viewpoint
  • Display, distribute, or post third-party materials (flyers, posters, banners, social media graphics, promotional items) containing Little Diggers branding alongside political messages, partisan symbols, candidate/party names, advocacy language, or commercial endorsements
  • Conduct fundraising, voter registration drives, petitioning, or other advocacy activities on franchise premises that could reasonably be perceived as brand-endorsed
  • Tag, mention, or associate Little Diggers social media accounts, website, or location pages with political content, partisan events, or third-party endorsements in posts, stories, or advertisements

Permitted Activities (Strict Conditions — all must be met)

  • No Little Diggers branding appears in any third-party promotional, advocacy, or political materials
  • The activity is clearly identified as hosted or supported solely by the third party
  • No implication is created that the location or brand endorses the third party's position, candidate, cause, or product
  • Franchisee personnel do not participate in partisan advocacy during the activity

Example of permissible neutral participation: allowing space for generic community events, provided no brand identifiers are used in connection with any advocacy messaging.

Franchisee Obligations

  • Actively monitor and promptly remove any unauthorized third-party materials or statements that imply brand endorsement
  • Decline requests from third parties to use Little Diggers branding in connection with endorsements of any kind
  • Train staff to recognize and prohibit activities that could create the appearance of brand endorsement
  • Report any suspected unauthorized use of the brand by third parties to the Franchisor immediately

Exceptions

Any proposed arrangement involving third-party use of the brand in connection with an endorsement or affiliation requires prior written approval from the Little Diggers Corporate Office. Approvals are granted only in exceptional circumstances and will be documented.

Compliance & Enforcement

Compliance is a material obligation under the Franchise Agreement. Violations — including failure to prevent or correct unauthorized third-party endorsements — may result in remedial measures, up to and including termination of the franchise agreement. Report suspected violations immediately to the Franchisor's designated compliance contact.

Guests & Legal

Photo & Video Release Policy

Little Diggers Franchising, LLC ("Franchisor") and its franchisees may capture photographs, videos, and other media recordings ("Media") of guests, including children, for marketing, promotion, advertising, social media, website content, internal documentation, and brand enhancement.

To protect guest privacy — particularly that of minors — and comply with applicable laws, including considerations under the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) where relevant, this policy requires affirmative consent for Media use featuring identifiable individuals.

✍️Consent is incorporated into the standard guest waiver and release form. Guests (or parents/legal guardians for minors) are deemed to grant consent unless they affirmatively opt out in writing.

Consent Mechanism

  • All guests must complete and sign (or have signed on their behalf) the Little Diggers Guest Waiver and Release Form prior to entry or participation.
  • The waiver includes a clear Photo and Video Release section granting the franchisee, Franchisor, and their affiliates irrevocable, royalty-free permission to capture, use, reproduce, modify, distribute, and display Media featuring the guest (and any minor accompanying the guest) for the purposes described above, in any media now known or hereafter developed, without further approval, compensation, or notice.
  • Consent is granted by default upon signing the waiver without marking the opt-out provision.

Opt-Out Provision

  • Guests may opt out by clearly indicating their preference in writing. Opting out does not affect the guest's ability to access or participate in activities.
  • Franchisees must honor all opt-outs immediately and take reasonable measures to avoid capturing Media of opted-out individuals where feasible.

Franchisee Obligations

  • Ensure all waivers are current, clearly worded, and include the Photo and Video Release section with a prominent opt-out mechanism.
  • Train staff to explain the policy courteously upon request and to respect opt-out designations.
  • Maintain records of signed waivers (including opt-outs) per record retention requirements.
  • Refrain from using any Media that reasonably identifies an individual who has opted out.
  • Promptly remove or cease use of any Media upon receiving a post-capture opt-out or revocation request, where practicable.
  • Do not pressure guests to grant consent or imply that participation requires consent.

⚠️ Important Legal Notice

  • This policy and the waiver language must be reviewed and approved by a qualified local attorney in each franchisee's jurisdiction prior to implementation.
  • Franchisees are responsible for compliance with state, federal, and local laws regarding privacy, minors, consent, and media releases.
  • The Franchisor provides this policy as a guideline only and does not provide legal advice — consult independent legal counsel to customize the waiver and confirm its enforceability.
