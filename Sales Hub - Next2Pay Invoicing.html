What it does

The pitch: professional invoicing that gets you paid faster, from the same company that handles your payments.

Invoices & payment links

Send one-time or recurring invoices by email or text — the customer clicks and pays online. No more paper invoices, mailed checks or chasing.

Card + ACH / bank payments

Customers pay by credit card or straight from their bank account — ACH keeps costs low on big-ticket invoices, which is exactly where invoicing merchants feel fee pain.

Recurring billing & auto-pay

Set-and-forget billing schedules with cards or bank accounts on file — ideal for service contracts, memberships, retainers and rent-style billing.

Merchant dashboard & reporting

A branded portal where the merchant sends invoices, tracks who's paid and who hasn't, and reconciles deposits — with automatic payment reminders doing the chasing.

Who it's for

Contractors and home services, professional services (legal, accounting, consulting), medical & wellness billing, B2B suppliers, property and service companies — anyone who bills after the work instead of ringing a register.

Processing — two ways to run it

Cost Plus (IC+)
Interchange + your markup. The merchant pays true interchange plus the buy rates below plus whatever margin you set — transparent, great for larger / ACH-heavy billers.
Dual Pricing
0% to the merchant. The card-paying customer covers the fee, so the merchant nets the full invoice — the easiest pitch for most service billers.

The merchant picks one — Cost Plus (IC+) or Dual Pricing. Build it either way in the Proposal Builder.
