Who it's for

Perfect fit

Bodegas, convenience stores, smoke shops, small grocers and delis — high-volume, small-ticket retail where a purpose-built register with a customer-facing screen, scanner and cash-discount pricing wins on simplicity and cost.

When to pitch something else

Full-service restaurants (SkyTab by Shift4), serious retail inventory needs like liquor with age verification (Korona), or merchants who want deep add-ons and integrations (Quantic). Match the product to the business.
