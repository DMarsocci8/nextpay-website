Gift Card Program

How Shift4's gift card program works — the migration to the TCC platform, why merchants now place their own orders, and the step-by-step Gift Card Setup Wizard in Lighthouse BMS.

Migration & setup Placing an order The Setup Wizard Accepting cards Designing cards Pricing & takeaways

Migration & setup

In January 2022, Shift4 began converting merchants from It's Your Card (IYC) over to The Customer Connection's (TCC) gift card platform. The pilot started with single-owner locations; after the initial launch, Shift4 expanded the program to multi-location merchants.

Why it matters for the merchant: previously, Shift4 staff had to manually place gift card orders on a merchant's behalf. The new platform and portal let merchants place their own gift card orders and gives them a central location to manage all of their gift card needs.

Placing a gift card order

Merchants place gift card orders directly in Lighthouse BMS. If it's the merchant's first time placing a gift card order in Lighthouse BMS, they're taken through the Gift Card Setup Wizard — a short, guided flow that gets the program live.

The Gift Card Setup Wizard

The wizard walks the merchant through choosing card types, naming their store, branding it, and going live.

Card Types

First, the wizard prompts the merchant to choose whether they want to offer virtual (E-Gift) cards, physical gift cards, or both.

Gift Card Store

The merchant names their gift card store and creates a unique store URL (for example, mygiftcard.s4shops.com/gc/). This is how the gift card program links to Lighthouse Business Manager, so the merchant can place orders and edit their preferences. Even if they don't plan to sell online, they still need to fill in a shop name — and if they ever choose to sell eGift cards later, it's already set up for quick activation. The URL must be at least 4 characters long.

Add Logo

The merchant can add their logo to the online store. This step is not required and can be skipped or updated at a later time.

Select Colors

The merchant selects a primary and secondary color to be displayed in their gift card store. Colors can be changed later.

Go live & finish setup

Once the wizard is complete, the merchant's gift card store is live (e.g., shops.giftcard/<merchant>). They're then prompted to finish two more steps: Select Physical Card Design (choose how the cards look) and Order Inventory (make sure cards are in stock to sell).

E-Gift vs. physical gift cards

E-Gift Cards Digital

  • Sold online
  • Received by the recipient via email
  • Funds are instantly available for use
  • Redeemable at the POS via QR code or keying

Physical Gift Cards Printed

  • Can be sold online or in-store
  • Receive notice of new orders
  • Fulfill at the POS and mail to the customer
  • Redeemable at the POS by swipe, QR code, or keying

Accepting gift cards

Any merchant eligible to switch to TCC for gift card offerings receives an email with instructions on how to set it up in Lighthouse BMS. Once live, merchants can accept gift cards online and in person — three ways:

Physical gift cards In person

Process the payment using the attached MSR (magnetic swipe reader) or by manually entering the gift card number.

E-gift cards In person

Scan the QR code within the email the customer received, or manually enter the gift card number.

Online Web

The customer enters the gift card number into the payment screen of the merchant's website.

Legacy IYC cards Still valid

Merchants who were on the older IYC program can still accept previously sold IYC gift cards after switching to the new TCC program.

Designing & ordering cards

When the merchant clicks the prompt to select a physical card design, they can design their own card or select a design from the library. Only one design can be selected/ordered at a time — to order multiple designs, place each order separately.

Choosing to design their own card lets the merchant upload an image, upload their logo, add text, and select a background color. Once a design is selected, a preview of the front and back is displayed. When the merchant approves it, they move to the next step to select quantity, shipping method, and shipping address.

Approved orders are mailed out within 10–15 business days. During the holiday season, gift card orders ship within 15–20 business days.

Who fulfills what: for a physical gift card purchased online, the merchant is responsible for loading the correct amount onto the card and shipping it to the customer. An e-gift card purchased online sends the customer an email with a QR code and barcode for redemption — no shipping required.

Pricing & free trial

60-day free trial · then $12.95 / month + $0.20 / transaction

With enrollment, the merchant receives a 60-day free trial and an optional 50 free physical gift cards. After the 60-day free trial, a $12.95 monthly fee and a $0.20 per-transaction fee apply.

Why merchants offer gift cards — the pitch

Three benefits to lead with:

  • Immediate revenue return — cash in hand in advance of providing the product or service.
  • Improved word-of-mouth advertising from loyal customers.
  • Ease-of-use dashboard to create designs, re-order additional physical gift cards, and maintain existing cards and their balances.
