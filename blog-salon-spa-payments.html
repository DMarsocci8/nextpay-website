Salon & spa payments: no-shows, memberships and cards on file

The payment setup that protects your chair time — deposits, card-on-file, memberships and tips that flow to the right person.

A salon's inventory is time. An empty chair at 2pm is revenue you never get back — which is why the best salon payment setups are really no-show insurance with a card reader attached.

Stop no-shows with cards on file

The single biggest upgrade: take a card at booking and store it securely (tokenized, so you never hold the number). Pair it with a posted cancellation policy — 24 hours' notice or a fee — and no-shows drop fast. With Next2Pay, cards vault securely and a no-show fee is a two-tap charge, attached to the client record.

Deposits for high-value services

Color corrections, extensions, bridal — anything over an hour deserves a deposit. Send a payment request by text when the appointment books: the client taps, pays the deposit, and the balance settles at checkout. Text requests get seen and paid dramatically faster than emailed invoices.

Memberships are the growth engine

Monthly facials, blowout plans, massage memberships — recurring revenue smooths the calendar and survives slow weeks. Recurring billing runs the plan automatically: card or bank (ACH), auto-retry on declines, receipts emailed. Set the schedule once and the revenue shows up.

Tips without the drama

Tip prompts on the customer-facing screen raise tip rates and remove awkwardness. Make sure your POS supports tip-by-provider so payouts are clean — booth renters especially need their own reporting.

The stack we recommend

  • Front desk: Square or Clover station with customer-facing tips
  • Booking deposits & memberships: Next2Pay — invoicing, text-to-pay, recurring billing and card vault in one app
  • Fees: dual pricing so card-paying clients cover the processing cost, or a surcharge program where it fits
Booth renters: each renter can run their own NextPay account — separate deposits, separate reporting, no commingled money.
