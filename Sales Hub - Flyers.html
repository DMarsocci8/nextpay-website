Flyers & One-Pagers — Personalize & Print

Pick a flyer or postcard, add your name and contact info once, and print it yourself — full-size 8.5″ × 11″ flyers and 5″ × 7″ postcards, ready for any printer. Your details are saved on this device and shared with your business card, so you only type them once.

Printing tip: click Print / Save PDF, then set margins to None and scale to 100% (uncheck “Fit to page”). Choose “Save as PDF” to email it, or send straight to your printer on letter paper.

Adding more flyers

Have a flyer to add? A Canva export as a 2× PNG (8.5×11, with the name/contact area left blank) — or a designed HTML flyer — gets added to the picker above with your contact block dropped in the right spot.
