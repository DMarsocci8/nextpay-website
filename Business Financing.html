FAQ

Questions borrowers ask before applying

What funding options are available?
Equipment financing, revenue-based financing, term loans, business lines of credit, short-term real-estate loans, and long-term programs such as DSCR and commercial real-estate financing.
Can one application handle business and real-estate funding?
Yes. The application starts by asking which type of funding you're applying for, then branches into the fields and documents required for that path.
What documents does a business borrower provide?
Company details, owner details, the requested amount, current obligations, and the most recent three months of bank statements.
What's required for real-estate deals?
Property information, credit and experience questions, deal numbers, and a broader document set such as ID, bank statements, purchase contract, mortgage statement, insurance, photos, and budget materials.
How fast can approvals or funding move?
Several programs are positioned around approvals within about 24 hours, with some working-capital products faster; certain real-estate programs close over a multi-day to multi-week window depending on structure.
Term loan vs. line of credit — what's the difference?
A term loan provides a set amount with a defined repayment schedule. A line of credit is revolving capital — you draw what you need and pay only for the amount actually used.

Financing programs are subject to underwriting, business qualifications, and product availability by market.
