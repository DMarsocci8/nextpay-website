The best POS setup for a food truck

Small counter, no room for error: what actually matters in a truck — speed, battery, connectivity — and the setups that deliver it.

A food truck is the harshest POS environment there is: spotty internet, no counter space, a line that has to move, and a battery that has to last a shift. Here's what matters and what we recommend.

What a truck actually needs

  • Speed at the window — tap-to-pay and tips on one screen, no second device
  • Connectivity that survives a parking lot — 4G/LTE with offline fallback
  • All-day battery — or a handheld that charges in its cradle between rushes
  • Simple menu edits — 86 an item from the line in seconds

The full-service pick: SkyTab by Shift4

A restaurant-grade handheld with pay-at-the-window, commission-free online ordering and QR pay. If your truck runs like a restaurant — modifiers, combos, a real kitchen flow — this is the one. Plan for a couple of weeks of setup: menu build, install and training are included and worth doing right.

The simple picks: Square and SumUp

Square Mobile is the classic first-truck setup: an app, a reader, running in an afternoon. SumUp is the budget champion — a $54 reader or the $249 palm-size Terminal with built-in printer and no monthly software fee, with a full register bundle at $499 when you're ready.

Don't forget the terminal option

Plenty of trucks skip the POS entirely and run a smart terminal — a PAX A920 Pro or Dejavoo QD4 handheld takes cards anywhere, prints receipts, and doubles as a backup when the main system (or the venue Wi-Fi) goes down.

Fee tip: dual pricing and cash discount work beautifully at a walk-up window — post cash prices, let card payers cover the card fee, and process at effectively 0%. It's legal in all 50 states when displayed correctly, and every setup above supports it.

Bottom line

Full menu and real volume: SkyTab. First season or simple menu: Square or SumUp. Either way, add a handheld terminal as backup — the day the internet dies at a festival, it pays for itself.
